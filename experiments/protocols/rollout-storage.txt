Direct rollout storage — prospective protocol, 2026-10-15

Objective remains complete single-GPU training throughput, not an isolated copy
microbenchmark. Run019's prepared1/2/4 curve improved only11.9% at4096streams,
below20%; four cells hit unchanged deadlines. Every result remains evidence.
Run018 measured substantial batch assembly and input materialization. Source
inspection shows observations copied into per-row pending objects, then copied
again into final arrays with nested Python loops.

Intervention: allocate one final RolloutBatch per collection, write sampled
observations/actions/values/distributions into their final [time,stream] slots,
and finalize reward/done/count when the next learner decision or terminal arrives.
Replace per-row pending objects and the final nested copy loop. Use an array mask
to select pending learner rows in the same ascending order. No changes to native
rules, inference shapes/order, sampling RNG, learning rules or checkpoint format.
No returned batch is overwritten or reused by subsequent collection.
