Persistent CPU producers feeding one GPU — prospective protocol, 2026-10-15

Objective: clear the inference-serving bottleneck in complete single-GPU training.
Run018 attributes about half the large-stream collection host window to native
stepping plus observation encoding; prepared model forwarding is about8%. These
profiled fractions motivate the intervention, not a promised throughput gain.
Run017's1.844x isolated serving gain and0.82% ordinary training gain remain evidence
against accepting another isolated optimization. Its strict parity failure remains
unresolved, including baseline-to-baseline numerical variation.

each owning disjoint native streams and contiguous observation-buffer row views.

seed strides preserve resets across shard counts. No changes to rules, learning,
precision, stream count or policy update cadence. workers1 is the direct native
path. All owners close on completion/failure; recovery uses the existing global
collector snapshot. Local tests prove uneven-shard, auto-reset, rollout, learning
and recovery parity; they do not establish GPU scaling.
