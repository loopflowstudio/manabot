## Retained chapter and measures

**Faithful Play and Training** (`35bbc647-c7e4-450b-9ef7-108628b39335`) was approved on 2026-09-24, with a review point of **2026-10-08**, not a promised success deadline. Exact original scope, baseline evidence and dispositions live in the [chapter record](../../chapters/20260924T233419Z-042de726/start.md). The outstanding measures remain evidence obligations, not instructions to restart opening work.

1. **Play the intended rules.** Establish the selected format and eligible Lesson-pool setup and implement the full Learn choice, including the outside-game Lesson path where available, optional discard/draw, and decline. Exercise it through the real human and agent paths with correct visibility and deterministic replay. Reproduce and fix further selected-matchup discrepancies; a deliberately reduced mechanic is not faithful to the agreed world.

2. **Meet real consumer budgets.** Jointly with Intelligence, freeze representative training/play consumers, machine/build profiles, throughput/latency/RSS measures and budgets before accepting performance. Measure current behavior; remove only demonstrated blockers and verify the same workloads and semantics afterward. Adequate existing performance may satisfy a budget without new optimization. Preserve negative results; do not restart deferred calibration or general provider work by inertia.

3. **Keep one exact authority.** Human Commands, bot actions, replay and search forks actually required by the chosen agent use consistent versioned rules and identities. On a prospectively declared complete-game cohort, executing the same recorded Commands from equivalent roots reproduces state witnesses and ordered consequences across consumers; independently acting humans and bots need not choose the same moves. Retain executable witnesses for stale/duplicate rejection, deterministic replay, viewer privacy and fork/root isolation where applicable. Include Lesson-pool state and visibility in the contract. Changed mechanics require renewed evidence for affected claims; frozen evidence stays intact and a defect does not automatically invalidate every run. No consumer rebuilds its own rules.

Game owns comfortable controls and human completion; Intelligence owns training and strength claims. Agreement between consumers is not by itself proof that a mechanic follows the intended rules.
