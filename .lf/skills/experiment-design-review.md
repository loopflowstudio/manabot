Review whether the proposed experiment can support its intended claim.

Check controls, intervention axes, matching compute, seeds, splits, deck function,
world/input admission, evidence completeness and the declared analysis. Inspect
required exposure evidence: evaluation draws/actions cannot silently substitute
for training opportunities or resolutions. Distinguish proposed numbers and lists
from accepted settings. A software check is not empirical validation.

Use the bound loop decision protocol: advance only with a sufficiently specified
accepted design, iterate for concrete correctable defects, or block on missing
consequential judgment. Preserve supplied approvals without asking again. When
no person is available, record the unresolved judgment; do not approve it by
elapsed time. Design acceptance supplies no new execution or spending authority.
