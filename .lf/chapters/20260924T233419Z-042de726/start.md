## Game — complete games worth playing

Retain Project 9106931a-3e9a-48ef-881b-30ee3363f929 (currently Play, Replay, and Study); proposed display name **Complete Games**.

Definition: Make the existing Allies versus Lessons demo a game the User can follow, express every intended legal play in, finish against an identified trained opponent, and choose to play again.

1. **Understand each change and next decision.** In real human games, the User can follow casting, targeting, resolution, combat, damage, death, and turn changes and identify whose decision is next. Reproduce reported confusion in exact versioned positions, render from authoritative semantic events, retain regression coverage, and validate the fix through human play, including skip/fast-forward and reduced-motion behavior. No known transition blocker remains at closure. Existing event code alone is insufficient proof.
2. **Express every intended legal play without awkward controls.** Maintain an explicit interaction-family and known-friction ledger for the selected matchup, including large and rare choice sets and the full Learn/Lesson interaction. Every reported awkward action blocks closure until reproduced, fixed and confirmed. Preserve complete legal choices and server Command authority; record exercised and untested coverage. Grouping/staged choice is a design candidate, not a mandated solution.
3. **Finish and choose to return.** The User completes games against clearly identified trained checkpoints and voluntarily chooses to play again. Retain all attempted sessions, including abandonment, with match, world, opponent, deck/seat and reproducible Command identities; discover and remove additional stopping friction through those sessions. Agree the concrete human-play cohort before scoring it, revisit repaired interactions across later sessions, and exercise both deck assignments. Automated terminal fixtures do not substitute for this human outcome.

## Intelligence — train a human challenger

Retain Project 73884527-4c81-4682-aa52-25fb586f08f9 (currently Search Teacher and Student Arena); proposed display name **Trained Challengers**.

Definition: Make training reproducible and useful: produce versioned manabots for the selected matchup, establish full-game improvement at declared cost, and demonstrate a repeatable challenge to the User under a protocol fixed before scoring.

1. **Repeat training through playable checkpoints.** On the corrected frozen world, a documented bounded workflow succeeds across repeated complete executions, exporting checkpoints that load through the real demo and complete games with both deck assignments. Pin code, content/rules, observation/action ABI, configuration, data and seed identities, checkpoint digest and training/inference cost receipts. Declare the reproducibility contract; do not imply byte-identical stochastic training unless promised. Account for every attempt and failure.
2. **Show full-game improvement.** Freeze the corrected world, current demo-opponent baseline, controls, independent training seeds, held-out paired deals, deck/seat assignments, training budgets, matched inference compute, cohort and analysis before evaluation. Compare across complete games, report uncertainty, legality, information safety, competence failures and total cost, and demonstrate improvement against the predeclared criterion. Account for every candidate attempt and exclusion. A cheaper feasibility run or an honest negative result informs the next decision but does not satisfy an improvement claim.
3. **Beat the human repeatably.** Agree and record candidate identity, inference budget, deck/seat assignments, deal handling, session window, assistance policy, sample/stopping rule, uncertainty treatment, interruptions and success criterion before counting results. Then evaluate that frozen candidate across the complete scheduled cohort against the User and meet the criterion. Retain every attempt; candidate/world changes require a new cohort. An isolated win, a cherry-picked checkpoint, a constructed terminal fixture, or a loss caused by awkward controls is insufficient.

## Rules — faithful games and a usable training world

Retain Project 35bbc647-c7e4-450b-9ef7-108628b39335 (currently Search and Study Runtime Prototype); proposed display name **Faithful Play and Training**.

Definition: Give human play and training one faithful, reproducible Allies versus Lessons world, supporting the intended card interactions and actual consumer workloads without divergent rules or hidden-state leakage.

1. **Play the intended rules.** Establish the selected format and eligible Lesson-pool setup and implement the full Learn choice, including the outside-game Lesson path where available, optional discard/draw, and decline. Exercise it through the real human and agent paths with correct visibility and deterministic replay. Reproduce and fix further selected-matchup discrepancies; a deliberately reduced mechanic is not faithful to the agreed world.
2. **Meet real consumer budgets.** Jointly with Intelligence, freeze representative training/play consumers, machine/build profiles, throughput/latency/RSS measures and budgets before accepting performance. Measure current behavior; remove only demonstrated blockers and verify the same workloads and semantics afterward. Adequate existing performance may satisfy a budget without new optimization. Preserve negative results; do not restart deferred calibration or general provider work by inertia.
3. **Keep one exact authority.** Human Commands, bot actions, replay and search forks actually required by the chosen agent use consistent versioned rules and identities. On a prospectively declared complete-game cohort, executing the same recorded Commands from equivalent roots reproduces state witnesses and ordered consequences across consumers; independently acting humans and bots need not choose the same moves. Retain executable witnesses for stale/duplicate rejection, deterministic replay, viewer privacy and fork/root isolation where applicable. Include Lesson-pool state and visibility in the contract. Changed mechanics invalidate old compatible-looking evidence until rebound and checked; no consumer rebuilds its own rules.
