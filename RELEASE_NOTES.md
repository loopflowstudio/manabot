# v0.2.5

<!-- loopflow:release-notes=narrative;gate=safe -->

This release brings full Apple Silicon MLX training parity alongside major MLX and Torch MPS speedups, replaces dense v11 rollout buffers with typed ragged `ObservationBatch` transport, and expands `managym` with over 370 new cards across Foundations, Adventures, Omens and modal faces. It also adds live `GameSnapshot` branching and native multi-worker snapshot self-play, explicit vector-slot parking, opening-hand mulligan play, and upgrades the Etude dependency to `etude-sdk>=0.4.7,<0.5`.

## Full MLX parity, portable continuation and Apple Silicon speedups

Apple Silicon training now supports the full model, update and belief surface on MLX without importing PyTorch, and tracked self-play runs can stop and continue across Torch and MLX from one portable save layout.

- **Complete MLX architecture, PPO, live-token and belief parity:** MLX now trains every `ActorCritic` configuration (`categorical_wdl` critics, all four value aggregations, `attention_on=False`, hashed record vocabularies, `recent_events`, belief rows and GRU/transformer `decision_memory`), both `damped_policy` and `ppo` updates, packed `live_tokens` on `mps` and `cpu`, and portable `belief.safetensors` sampling and fitting across all five shipped recipes. ([#575](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/575), [#585](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/585), [#596](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/596))
- **Portable cross-backend tracked continuation:** Both Torch and MLX now save and resume tracked self-play using one four-file layout (`state.json`, `continuation.json`, `learner.safetensors`, `sampling.safetensors`), preserving raw and EMA weights, Adam moments and per-parameter clocks, cumulative counts and in-flight native games across Torch and MLX. ([#595](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/595))
- **Faster MLX and Torch MPS updates and inference:** Shared host observation preparation, referenced-only catalog encoding on Apple Silicon, a custom packed GRU backward pass and compact bucketed attention on MLX, live fact routing on Torch MPS, and power-of-two MPS inference attention padding cut training and collection overhead across Apple Silicon devices while preserving parity. ([#583](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/583), [#586](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/586), [#592](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/592), [#597](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/597), [#598](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/598))

## Typed ragged observations, live snapshots and explicit slot parking

V11 observations now move and stay as compact, typed CSR batches, while native vector slots park on terminal games and support detached mid-game branching across threads and workers.

- **Typed ragged `ObservationBatch` transport:** `managym.encode_observation_batch`, `VectorEnv.set_observation_layout` / `step_observation_batch`, and `RolloutPool.set_observation_layout` / `step_active_batch` replace fixed-capacity zero-padded `float32` rollout boxes with immutable typed CSR spans (`int32` IDs/joins/enums, `uint8` flags, `float32` scalars), cutting retained v11 rollout storage ~45× and peak process RSS by 45% in paired runs. ([#589](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/589))
- **Detached `GameSnapshot` branching and native snapshot self-play:** Scalar and vector games can save thread-safe `GameSnapshot` handles, restore independent continuations with sibling library sharing, and export private snapshot replays that Academy can open. `SelfPlay.snapshot` runs directly on native `VectorEnv` workers with a resumable `SnapshotBank`, horizon-bounded continuations and grouped RLOO baselines. ([#577](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/577), [#578](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/578), [#581](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/581), [#594](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/594))
- **Explicit vector-slot parking and `mimalloc` default:** Native `VectorEnv` and `RecordedVectorEnv` now park finished and failed slots with chooser `-1` until callers explicitly refill them through `load_slots_into_buffers`. `mimalloc` is now the default Rust allocator for native `managym` builds, improving multi-worker collection throughput across macOS and Linux. ([#587](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/587), [#576](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/576))

## Foundations card coverage, unified casting and opening hands

Engine rules expand to cover opening-hand mulligans, a single casting authority for split/modal/inset spells and overlapping grants, sequential combat-damage choices with first strike, and unified per-turn history.

- **Unified casting authority and ~90 new cards:** Every cast and land play now resolves through one `(object, spell profile, play route)` authority covering Adventures, Omens, prepared spells, modal double-faced backs and overlapping casting permissions (Omniscience, Muldrotha, Zul Ashur, Tinybones, Etali, Gandalf), completing TDM's Omens. ([#580](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/580))
- **Foundations combat, damage and turn-history coverage:** Two Foundations passes add 207 first-printing cards with sequential combat-damage assignment, a dedicated first-strike damage window and replacement-order choices, followed by unified `PlayerTurnHistory`, damage-pair history and `PendingTrigger.triggering_amount` adding 108 more definitions (catalog 814 → 922). ([#591](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/591), [#599](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/599))
- **Opening-hand mulligans and explicit draw reporting:** Both seats now play through keep/mulligan, private bottom ordering and opening-hand pregame actions before turn one. Simulation drops its life-total fallback and reports engine-committed `draws` separately from timeouts. ([#582](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/582))

## Operational and compatibility notes

- **Dependencies:** `manabot` now requires `etude-sdk>=0.4.7,<0.5` (up from `0.4.6` / `0.4.5`; no database migration needed). Etude 0.4.7 adds owned SSH host recovery, `etude queue --resume`/`--watch`, cross-experiment `Report.include`, and warm-start clock displacement. ([#579](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/579), [#595](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/595))
- **Breaking vector-slot lifecycle:** Native `managym.VectorEnv` and `RecordedVectorEnv` no longer auto-reset finished slots; stepping past a terminal slot without masking or calling `load_slots_into_buffers` raises an error. `auto_reset`, `replace_failed_into_buffers` and `reassign_roots_into_buffers` are removed. ([#587](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/587))
- **`prepare` layout API and belief sampler format:** `manabot.policy.prepare.prepare` now takes explicit `attention` and `facts` layout arguments instead of boolean flags. Belief samplers save as portable `belief.safetensors` (Torch keeps a read fallback for legacy `.pt` samplers; MLX requires `.safetensors`). ([#598](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/598), [#596](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/596))
- **Deprecations aligned for `0.3.0`:** Dense observation transport (`VectorEnv.set_buffers`, `RolloutPool.set_buffers`, `encode_observation_into`, `encode_observations_into`, `ObservationLayout.axis_extents`, `ObservationLayout.object_references`), `CapturedVector` and `InMemorySnapshotVector` now emit `DeprecationWarning` targeting `0.3.0`. All prior `0.3.0`/`0.4.0` deprecation warnings are aligned to `0.3.0` ahead of the upcoming `0.3.0` cleanup release. ([#581](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/581), [#589](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/589))
- **Policy inputs and state hashes:** Policy input v11 appends columns for opening-turn entry, first-strike damage, casting profiles and turn history while remaining backward-compatible with saved v11 checkpoints via saved column prefixes. Exact-state hash contracts advance from v17 to v31. ([#580](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/580), [#582](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/582), [#591](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/591), [#599](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/599))
