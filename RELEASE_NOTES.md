# v0.2.4

<!-- loopflow:release-notes=narrative;gate=safe -->

This release packages Academy directly inside `managym` with a redesigned interactive board and unified game recording, completes the TLE bonus sheet with richer mana-payment controls and CR 400.7 object identity, and unifies observation encoding, portable `.safetensors` checkpoints and `etude train` recipes across `manabot`. Upgrade for standalone Academy play and replay, full 1,000-pool TLA trophy coverage, and checkpoints that remain playable across card and column additions; training scripts and custom Python callers need the migration steps below.

## Install and play Academy from managym alone

Academy no longer requires a repository checkout, Node, Rust or `manabot` to play against built-in bots or inspect saved games. Play, Replay, Watch and Study now share one full-window board and one recording format.

- **Standalone `managym[academy]` distribution:** `managym` wheels now bundle the Academy server, static Svelte client and curated packs; `managym academy` starts the table against Random, Passive and Search without installing Torch. Installing matching `manabot` exposes trained-checkpoint and local Qwen providers through `managym.bots` entry points. ([#517](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/517), [#548](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/548))
- **One interactive board for Play, Replay, Watch and Study:** Cards act on click or open a menu when several offers apply, combat can be drafted and natively previewed before committing one creature at a time, priority stops add **Once** beside **Off** and **Every time**, and Play carries setup across **Rematch**, **Swap sides** and **Change opponent**. Hovering or focusing casts, abilities or payment choices opens a native **Payment preview** showing exact taps and leftover mana before committing. ([#560](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/560), [#545](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/545), [#538](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/538))
- **Unified game recording, replay and live reducers:** `managym.recording` (`ScalarCapture`, `CapturedVector`) and `managym.replay.walk` record and re-execute games across scalar, vector, training and evaluation producers without storing boards, feeding `CardPerformance`, `Behaviour` and custom `Reducer` statistics in one pass. `managym academy replay <file|dir>` opens complete, interrupted or diverged recordings with only `managym` installed, and `managym.diagnostics` adds opt-in pre-call journals and stall watchdogs. ([#553](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/553), [#540](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/540))

## Richer rules, mana control and card coverage

Engine upgrades finish the TLE bonus sheet, give players and bots explicit control over mana tapping and floating, and enforce generation-checked object identity and library knowledge across play and search.

- **All 61 TLE bonus-sheet cards and 67 additional cost-payment cards:** Adding Release to Memory, Lita, Mechanical Engineer and Heartbeat of Spring admits all 1,000 retained TLA trophy pools as playable (up from 995 in 0.2.3), alongside 67 new cost-payment cards and shared Foundations payment mechanics. ([#512](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/512), [#511](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/511), [#562](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/562))
- **Manual source tapping, smarter autotapping and per-seat priority floats:** Casting or activating now offers useful fixed-source taps beside automatic `PayCost`, while automatic payment prunes surplus taps and prefers plans that keep follow-up hand and battlefield abilities affordable. Casting costs and receipts live in one payment transaction, and payment modes and priority floats (`off`, `usable`, `always`) are configured independently per seat. ([#554](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/554), [#524](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/524), [#559](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/559), [#562](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/562))
- **CR 400.7 object appearances, known library positions and native heuristic:** Every zone appearance is now a generation-checked `ObjectId` backed by a physical `CardId`, keeping tokens and spell copies out of card-only operations and rejecting stale references. Public and private library positions persist across play, policy inputs and possible-world search; the heuristic opponent now runs natively in `managym` via `player_policies=("agent", "heuristic")` in scalar, vector and arena games; and bounded per-game payment caching speeds up large-board payment search while preserving nested Waterbend reservations. ([#491](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/491), [#525](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/525), [#550](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/550), [#518](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/518), [#551](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/551))

## One observation pipeline, portable checkpoints and Etude training

Observation encoding, policy preparation, checkpoint persistence and recipe execution now share single authorities, letting checkpoints stay playable across engine growth and giving training runs finer combat and card telemetry.

- **Sole native encoder and one-token v11 inputs:** The duplicate Python observation encoder is deleted; `managym.ObservationLayout` and `managym.encode_observation_into` own all buffer allocation, validation and writing, publishing named columns and row kinds through `managym.observation_layout()`. Policy input **v11** becomes the fresh default—one token row per visible card, permanent, spell or stack ability—while deprecated v9/v10 remain for earlier checkpoints. ([#521](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/521), [#566](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/566))
- **Portable `.safetensors` checkpoints and per-column input readings:** Training saves policies as `.safetensors` over shared host-prepared `Prepared` inputs, recording ordered input columns, `input_readings`, definition keys, card names and writer provenance. Compatible older checkpoints can now be seated on current decks (reading saved column prefixes and stored card programs), `training_corpus(...)` queries completed training games in DuckDB, and fresh models derive readings from native column kinds (`one_hot:n`, `steps:8`, `log`, `as_is`) with `stream_norm` and `focus_film` enabled in the baseline and Elemental recipes. ([#519](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/519), [#558](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/558), [#567](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/567), [#568](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/568))
- **Etude recipe execution, combat probes and updated training defaults:** Recipes are now Python modules with `train(config)` launched and resumed via `etude train <file>`. Self-play adds closed and mixed decklist training (`UniformDecks.decklists`), opt-in whole-game opponent decision memory (off by default after the pilot found no strength gain at 7–8M decisions), post-update combat board-dependence probes and 11 checkpoint behaviour curves, an opt-in forward-KL floor (`reference_forward_kl`) alongside a `tau_scale=0.1` baseline default after the TLA trophy floor study, grouped card-program gradients (2.17× faster CUDA PPO updates on an L4), automatic BF16 for wide GPU models (`hidden_dim >= 256`), and automatic card-evidence capture on every `play_pairing` benchmark. ([#500](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/500), [#547](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/547), [#555](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/555), [#570](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/570), [#571](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/571), [#514](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/514), [#520](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/520), [#572](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/572))

## Operational and compatibility notes

- **Dependencies:** `manabot` now requires `etude-sdk>=0.4.4,<0.5` alongside `torch>=2.15.0`. The `notebook` extra is removed. Run `uv run etude database upgrade` when moving an existing Etude launcher home to the single-database client. ([#539](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/539), [#500](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/500), [#570](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/570))
- **`manabot train` retired:** Run recipes with `uv run etude train <file>` (for example `manabot/recipes/local.py` or `manabot/recipes/baseline.py`). The old executor (`manabot/training/execution.py`, `TrainingRegime`, `manabot/experiment/` runners and `run.json` readers) is removed; reproduce frozen experiments from the commit named in their headers. ([#500](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/500))
- **Explicit backends and `Prepared` inputs:** Library callers in training, simulation, belief inference and verification must pass a framework backend or `BackendPolicy` explicitly rather than relying on implicit Torch imports, and Torch forwards accept only `manabot.policy.prepare.Prepared` batches. MLX supports policy inference, scalar damped updates and explicit `bfloat16` compute (float16 removed), and refuses `v11`, `stream_norm` and `focus_film`. ([#519](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/519), [#532](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/532), [#513](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/513), [#568](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/568))
- **Typed capacity errors, `play_pairing` return type and resume checks:** Capacity overflows from `encode_observation_into`, `encode_observations_into`, `Env.encode_observation` and `policy_action_count` now raise `managym.ObservationCapacity` (a `ValueError` subclass). `manabot.eval.pairing.play_pairing` now returns `etude.eval.result.Result` instead of `Tables` (direct callers index `.tables`). World labels are omitted from new checkpoints, recordings and v6 captures; runs saved before `input_readings` and `Counts.model` cannot `--resume` into a fresh model (warm-start from their last published policy instead). ([#566](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/566), [#572](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/572), [#558](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/558), [#567](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/567))
- **Deprecations:** New warning aliases scheduled for removal in `0.4.0` cover `academy.*` imports, `managym.WORLD_VERSION`, `Env.encode_observation*`, layout-less `set_buffers`, `ObservationSpace.encode` / `space.encoder` / `ObservationEncoder`, `PlayerConfig.disclosure`, flat characteristic getters on observations, `manabot export-game`, `CheckpointVillain`, `TrainingGames` / `record_games`, and `manabot.env.{failures,replay,card_capture}` / `manabot.infra.journal` re-exports. Existing aliases marked for `0.3.0` (`LearningCatalog`, `learning_catalog`, `opponent_policy`, etc.) remain present in `0.2.4`. ([#517](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/517), [#518](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/518), [#547](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/547), [#553](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/553), [#558](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/558), [#566](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/566))

- Spell copies now record their initial stack entry at incarnation zero so card-evidence capture follows copied spells through resolution instead of rejecting completed games. ([#573](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/573))
- Exhaustive effect-witness vocabulary checks catch missing runtime learning tokens before a game reaches them and name missing `(kind, hex)` keys in policy admission errors. ([#534](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/534))
- `VectorEnv.write_observations_for_players` reads fixed-seat observations across batched games without advancing play. ([#529](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/529))
- `bench_states` adds 17 repeatable large-board stress workloads with deterministic work counts and an `--allocations` diagnostic mode. ([#515](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/515), [#551](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/551))
- Academy switched its interface font from Work Sans to DM Sans and keeps compact trained-bot identities readable at laptop widths. ([#569](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/569), [#530](https://github.com/loopflowstudio/manabot/pull/530))
