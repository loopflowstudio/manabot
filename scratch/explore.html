Vision A · Selected starting approach

The reference league

Every batch joins a small, comprehensible league of deliberately built decks.

An experiment author chooses functional lists, checks their mana and game plan, and freezes the population. We can explain every training game’s origin. New cards get room without erasing old skills.

Exploration illustration: 50% retained · 30% new batch · 20% mixed. The executable proxy proposes 50/25/25; neither ratio is an accepted experimental setting.

manifest → integer tickets → UniformDecks
         → TrainingRegime → TrainingJob
         → checkpoint + fixed evaluation

The failure this avoids: a changed score caused by a changed game distribution that nobody can reconstruct.

Cost / risk. Small protocol changes; recurring human deck work. Authors can accidentally teach a handful of archetypes and miss rare mechanics. A 30% new-deck share means about 51% of games include at least one new-deck seat—not that the new mechanic is used.

Inspect the proposed evidence contract

Freeze ordered deck identities, unique population-ticket IDs, seed, world, vocabulary, input shape and split. Report actual draws and decisions separately from promised sampling mass. Authoring selects study decks; it never gates native card support.

Old anchor decklists and deal families stay fixed. A rules/world change requires new admitted controls in that world; raw before/after win rates cannot isolate learning.

Vision B · Breadth by construction

The coverage workshop

Every mechanic family gets functional games without someone writing every deck.

A generator builds constrained decks, screens them through ordinary games and freezes a diverse population. The author specifies the construction rules and synergy roles; the generator supplies the lists.

proposed construction envelope
  60 cards · 1–2 colors · 24–27 lands
  ≥12 plays costing ≤3 · ≥6 interaction cards
  explicit synergy partners + mana-source checks

candidate → native admission → functional screen
          → frozen population → existing training

Example constraints, not a universal deck-quality rule. Legality, functionality and competitive strength are separate.

The failure this avoids: “the card was in the deck” being mistaken for “the agent learned to use it.”

Cost / risk. Generator and exposure instrumentation, plus screening games. An illustrative screen is 16 candidates × 4 legs = 64 games per revision. Functional decks can still produce strategically empty games. This requires more plumbing than the current fixed-mixture path.

Inspect the proposed coverage receipt
family: adventure
present in deck: recorded
drawn / action offered / chosen / resolved: separate counts
decision quality: not inferred from a positive count
missing instrumentation: reported as unavailable
held-out combinations: excluded before generation

Coverage can propose the next manifest revision, but cannot silently alter a running experiment. Freeze screen opponents and seeds separately from final evaluation. Rejected study candidates remain ordinary playable cards.

Vision C · Radical success

The discovering league

The system discovers interactions and weaknesses that nobody thought to teach.

A bounded challenger searches training-only decks and opponents. At declared checkpoints, it proposes the next curriculum from observed weaknesses. A fixed replay share keeps old skills in the picture.

checkpoint → bounded challenge search
           → candidate evidence + rejection reasons
           → freeze next curriculum generation
           → 50% retained games / 50% challenges

sealed final test ── never visible to the search

Invented discoveries illustrate the mechanism; none has been observed here.

The failure this avoids: endlessly practicing what our deck authors already know how to test.

Cost / risk. Highest engineering and evaluation cost; a new research scope. An illustrative round screens 64 candidates × 4 legs = 256 games, before training. It may find deck imbalance or engine bugs instead of policy weaknesses. Total rounds and spending still need explicit bounds.

Inspect the proposed challenge receipt
parent checkpoint: immutable identity
candidate population: training split only
selection rule: frozen before screening
evidence: seat-swapped games + fixed-opponent controls
engine/capacity failure: retain replay, route to owner
next generation: new digest, never rewrite previous one

Use a static-mixture control at matched total compute. An adaptive win-rate curve alone cannot establish that the learning strategy improved.
