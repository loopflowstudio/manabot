# MTG-422 · Game procedure and terminal outcomes

Start at native reset. Either player can mulligan, choose the order of cards going to the bottom, and take opening-hand actions before the first turn. Time Stop and Sundial of the Infinite use the same route into cleanup. Poison, life, failed draws and explicit card instructions produce one authoritative result for play, search and replay.

This is the working-phase design at `0b1cc9cfef9c1a98430c825b9764a49237182696`. The six Foundations rows below are the non-planeswalker cards whose **primary** batch is `game_procedure`. Hare Apparent and Bloodthirsty Conqueror are dependency references in other primary batches. The five loyalty rows belong to Milestone 2 Task 06. 2. **Turn ending (implemented in `cc3671bd4`, simplified in `f2caa4fde`).** Reuse `Effect::EndTheTurn` in `state/src/ability.rs` and the existing Sundial path through `flow/resolution.rs`. Make `Game::end_the_turn` advance the native procedure in CR 725 order. Discard triggers waiting before the procedure, exile stack objects including the resolving spell, then run SBAs to stability before leaving the step. Allow mandatory SBA choices to suspend and resume with no priority or trigger placement. Preserve triggers created during termination for cleanup. End the actual step/phase, consume the current combat's control interval, drop this turn's queued combats, and preserve queued extra turns and future control. Enter cleanup even from cleanup. Use `turn_tick`'s normal discard-to-limit, simultaneous damage/expiry, SBA/trigger priority and repeated cleanup. Check interrupted frame completion, pending events, priority passes, combat capture and mana. Debug discriminator: a creature with lethal marked damage must die at the pre-cleanup check even if cleanup would otherwise clear that damage.

3. **Terminal arbitration (next slice).** Start with Fynn taking a player from eight to ten poison, with and without Herald. Follow the result from the SBA through Env reason and both seat rewards, then exercise simultaneous losses and a protected failed draw followed by Herald removal. Fold the optional opening workspace into OpeningHands / Playing / Finished as part of replacing writable player verdicts. Store one committed outcome with winning seat or draw and the causes needed by current consumers. One native operation evaluates immediate explicit win/loss instructions on resolution and simultaneous loss candidates at the SBA checkpoint. Read Herald's restrictions from effective characteristics at the relevant instant. A blocked instruction does nothing. Commit simultaneous unprotected losses as a draw independent of seat iteration. Check both seats against the same pre-batch state, including when Herald itself dies in that batch. Consume each attempted empty draw at its next SBA check, including a protected check. Life and poison persist. A protected player still receives poison and loses life. A blocked loss alone makes no SBA progress. Preserve mandatory legend choices in that state. Model poison as player counters, with public owner and exact count. Reuse `TriggerCondition::DealsCombatDamage`, the cost/event/frame authority and `Effect::TriggerModes` with `Modification::ModeChosen` for the card rows above.

   At commitment, retain every applicable cause per losing seat. Keep the existing single-reason Env projection deterministic: an empty draw and nonpositive life together report `deck_empty`, as in `finish_game_step` today. Add a simultaneous-cause fixture that reads the same outcome on the exact fork and after replay, and checks the legacy reason. A direct win carries its own cause without inventing a loss condition for the opponent.

4. **Decisions and control.** Use the ordinary native `Decision`, `Action` and semantic Command binding for each new choice. Keep `ActionSpace.player` as the chooser, `Decision.player()` as the rules subject and the active player as turn owner. Retain `TurnState.player_controls`, `Effect::ControlPlayer` and `Game::shares_information` for all three control sources. A skipped combat preserves pending next-combat control. Exercise a legal foretell or land play as a priority special action during delegated play. Pregame actions have no priority window. Neither a control effect nor a new procedure grants sideboard access or authority to concede for another seat.
