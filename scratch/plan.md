# MTG-420 · Battlefield forms, copies and persistent ability sources

The Task brief gives the design constraint: “Explore one-card transform, two-card meld, and hidden face-down characteristics against contrasting real cards before fixing the representation, preserving exact physical-card identity and viewer-safe observations.”

## Build the form probes first

| Constructed position | Witness that decides the representation |
| --- | --- |
| Cecil transforms in play; Esper Origins resolves via exile and enters transformed | Cecil keeps the same physical card, exact object, counters, attachments and targetability across transformation. Esper moves zones and acquires new exact identities. Keep its flashback, finality and Saga behavior. Preserve existing TLA transform sequencing, including the transform count used by a waiting ability. |
| Vanille with Fang, plus a namesake copy | From the first-main trigger, offer decline and legal payment. Check ownership and control. A genuine pair yields one targetable Ragnarok backed by two physical cards. A named copy can satisfy the name check but cannot meld. If the exiled pair cannot meld, the cards stay exiled. On death, resolve one permanent's death trigger and move both original cards. On return to an ordered library the owner chooses either order. Check the original's combined mana value and the zero mana value of a token copying the melded result. Exclude the result from deck entries. |
| Manifest Dread with two possible cards, then copy the manifested permanent | The chooser privately selects one of the two, the other reaches the graveyard. The battlefield object has public face-down characteristics. Its controller can inspect it and, for a creature front with payable cost, turn it face up as a special action. This preserves the object and counters. A copy gets the public 2/2 characteristics without gaining the hidden printing or the ability to turn up. Check a noncreature, a transforming creature, and one- and zero-card libraries. A face-down exile slot has different characteristics and inspection rights. |

Compare two designs against these witnesses before committing to the Rust API. Extending the existing front-face field alone can express Cecil but loses one physical card on Ragnarok's departure and gives a hidden printing to a copy. Prefer a battlefield form that records its backing card(s) and orientation, with a separate immutable value for what an effect can copy. Keep the common single-card path compact. Give tokens their own origin, including a transforming token's faces when the creating effect copies them. Preserve the rules distinction between a transforming token and a single-faced Clone copying the face currently up. Keep a single permanent row, even for meld. No fabricated physical card for a token or meld result. 2. The shared `CopiableValues` payload now feeds the departed-source read and token creation. Entry and token copies share exception application. 3. Author copy exceptions and live grants through `managym/state/src/{ability,effect}.rs`. Currently `Modification::apply_copiable` rejects `GrantTriggered`, although layers can grant a trigger to a live permanent. Support an executable intrinsic trigger for Electroduplicate's copy exception. Verify a copied printed death ability, an inherited end-step ability, a temporary granted death ability and the named Koma's Coil on a second copy. External scheduled sacrifices, such as The Fire Crystal's, watch only the created token. Keep ordinary counters, animation, equipment and transient grants out of the copied payload.
