MTG-508 Concept Review (Post-Implementation) · Coupled mana payments, fixed-tap choices, and owned activations

Area reviewed: Mana payment feasibility, execution, preview, and announced non-mana activation lifecycle across managym/src/flow/mana.rs, managym/src/flow/mana/{coupled,profile,help,preview,autotapper,interchangeable}.rs, managym/src/flow/decision.rs, managym/src/flow/payment.rs, managym/src/flow/play.rs, managym/src/flow/action.rs, managym/src/flow/game.rs, managym/src/flow/{layers,statics}.rs, managym/src/flow/interchangeable.rs, managym/src/agent/continuation.rs, and managym/src/state/hash.rs. This reviews the completed four-pass implementation at b0cd922864 against base bc4d2ac17f (freshly fetched origin/main at 606e79e90a is already an ancestor, with MTG-500 merged and MATCH_STATE_HASH_VERSION = 35 on origin/main vs. 36 on HEAD; no sync is needed).

Overall assessment: Passes 1 and 4 are conceptually clean—fixed-tap bonus choices fit natively into ManaActivationKind::FixedTap and produce_fixed_tap, and owned non-mana activations cleanly unify ActivationX, ActivatedTargeting, and Operation::Activation behind a borrow-friendly A = Box<ActivatedAbilityDefinition> parameter with acquired grant keys preserved in hash v36. Passes 2 and 3 implement the structured fork search cleanly for all committed fixtures, but four unfinished-rule gaps and one hot-path cost remain inside the coupled authority: (1) coupling() and the pure handoff misclassify non-tapping sensitive mana abilities, cost_reduction_per reads, and counted taps on stateful_tap boards; (2) resolution_mana_actions drops simple choice-tap lands on coupled boards when an automatic route exists; (3) PaymentCandidate::Spend skips the upfront life_budget check, and Delve SelectCard offers ignore graveyard-coupled sources; (4) PaymentModel::coupled() rescans sources and clones Game inside has_mutating_caller() on every call instead of classifying once per model.

Concrete interaction walk: where the built implementation breaks

Walk two minimal variations of Pass 3's own committed coupled boards through HEAD (b0cd922864):

  1. Non-tapping discounted mana ability (variation of coupled_fee_discount_reads_counters_removed_by_another_activation, coupled_boards.rs:402): Same board—White Lotus Tile granting ActivatedAbilitiesCostLess { predicate: creature with counter, generic: 1 }, Mountain ({R}), and a creature with a +1/+1 counter and two mana abilities:
    • Ability 0: {1}{R}, Pay 1 life: Add {G}{G} (no tap_source, discounted to {R} while it has a counter),
    • Ability 1: Remove a +1/+1 counter: Add {B},
    paying {G}{G}{B}. The legal order is Ability 0 first (funded by Mountain at the discounted {R} fee), then Ability 1.
    Failure in HEAD: coupling() (coupled.rs:98) checks ability.facts.reads || ability.tap_source && profile.sensitive_tap(). Because Ability 0 has tap_source == false and its discount comes from another permanent's StaticAbility::ActivatedAbilitiesCostLess (not ability.cost_reduction_per), coupling() classifies Ability 0 as Invariant. In expand() (line 681), Invariant abilities are skipped outside funding; the search tries only Ability 1 (Mutator) first, removes the counter, loses the discount (now {1}{R} with only Mountain), and falsely reports the legal payment unpayable. The exact same failure hits any non-tapping mana ability granted by a sacrificed rule source because loses_rules (line 592) only promotes fixed, not abilities.
  2. Delve exile beside a graveyard-reading mana source (variation of coupled_delve_counts_only_sacrifices_that_reach_the_graveyard, coupled_boards.rs:267): Cast a {1}{G}{G}{G} Delve spell with 1 card in graveyard, 1 Forest, and 1 mana source with Condition::GraveyardAtLeast { count: 1 } ({G}{G} if graveyard ≥ 1, else {G}).
    Failure in HEAD: First, PaymentModel::coupled() returns None (no sacrificing mana ability is present), so Delve is never treated as coupled. Second, help_actions() (payment.rs:1675) emits Action::SelectCard for every card in graveyard_payers without testing whether exiling it leaves the rest payable. Taking that offered exile drops the graveyard to 0 cards, lowers the source to {G}, leaves {G}{G}{G} owed with only {G}{G} available, and crashes with AgentError("empty non-terminal action space").
  3. Recovery / aligned model: Make coupling() promote any ability (tapping or not) to Sensitive when profile.writes_non_pt || loses_rules, scan cost_reduction_per in mana_ability_reads(), block the pure handoff on Obligation::Tap under stateful_tap(), and treat Delve exile as a real candidate step checked against post-exile feasibility whenever graveyard-sensitive rules exist.

Biggest mismatch: incomplete coupling classification vs. the closed whitelist contract

In coupled.rs and layers.rs, the role classifier and pure handoff omit non-tapping sensitive abilities, cost_reduction_per predicates, and counted taps under stateful_tap():

Only checks profile.sensitive_tap() when ability.tap_source is true; non-tapping abilities affected by writes_non_pt (static grants, ActivatedAbilitiesCostLess) or loses_rules fall into Invariant. loses_rules promotes fixed taps to Sensitive, but never promotes abilities. When profile.stateful_tap() is true, pure clears fixed/help taps but leaves source.untapped = true, so Obligation::Tap can still tap permanents inside pure.cached_plan. Scans condition, tap_cost, and output, but misses ability.cost_reduction_per.

Built at HEAD (b0cd922864)

  • coupling() promotes to Sensitive only on ability.facts.reads || ability.tap_source && profile.sensitive_tap(); loses_rules applies only to fixed.
  • mana_ability_reads() skips ability.cost_reduction_per.
  • pure handoff leaves source.untapped = true while stateful_tap() is active, allowing Obligation::Tap to mutate tap state inside the pure solver.
  • resolution_mana_actions omits || model.coupled().is_some() from explicit, dropping simple choice-tap lands when can_pay is true.
  • help_actions offers every Delve graveyard card unchecked; PaymentModel::coupled() ignores Delve when no sacrifice mana ability exists.
  • PaymentCandidate::Spend skips life_budget(player).can_pay(life_cost) before complete_mana_upfront.
  • model.coupled() rescans sources and clones Game via has_mutating_caller() on every call.

Aligned design (fixing the remaining gaps in this Task)

  • Promote any ability to Sensitive when ability.facts.reads || profile.writes_non_pt || loses_rules || (ability.tap_source && profile.stateful_triggers) (preserving the 207-state bound on the 10-Forest Citadel/Angel/Audience board where writes_non_pt is false).
  • Include ability.cost_reduction_per in mana_ability_reads().
  • Skip the pure handoff when profile.stateful_tap() and concrete contains Obligation::Tap (or clear source.untapped = false in pure when *fixed == Mutator).
  • Add || model.coupled().is_some() to explicit in resolution_mana_actions, matching compute_announced_mana_actions.
  • Treat Delve exile on graveyard-reading boards as coupled and filter help_actions / payment.rs:1250 through PaymentCandidate::DelveExile(ObjectId).
  • Share the life_budget + source-presence guard between fund_upfront and PaymentCandidate::Spend.
  • Compute coupling once on PaymentModel (and avoid constructing/scanning CoupledPayment or cloning Game in has_mutating_caller() on uncoupled boards).

Findings

Scope: This work (Pass 2/3 follow-up).

What breaks:

  • coupling() (coupled.rs:98) checks profile.sensitive_tap() only when ability.tap_source is true, and loses_rules (coupled.rs:592) only promotes fixed. Any non-tapping mana ability affected by ActivatedAbilitiesCostLess or a Layer-6 static grant/removal (writes_non_pt or loses_rules) is misclassified as Invariant and skipped in expand(), falsely rejecting legal payments.
  • mana_ability_reads() (layers.rs:641-671) never inspects ability.cost_reduction_per, so a mana ability whose generic cost reduction reads tapped fails to set profile.reads_tapped.
  • expand()'s pure handoff (coupled.rs:607-625) strips tapping mana sources when profile.stateful_tap() is true, but leaves source.untapped = true. A counted-tap obligation (Obligation::Tap) can therefore tap a permanent inside pure.cached_plan without recomputing a non-tapping Sensitive mana ability that reads tapped.

Recommendation: Pass loses_rules into coupling() and classify an ability as Sensitive whenever ability.facts.reads || profile.writes_non_pt || loses_rules || (ability.tap_source && profile.stateful_triggers); call reads.predicate(predicate) for ability.cost_reduction_per in mana_ability_reads(); clear source.untapped = false (or skip pure handoff when concrete has Obligation::Tap) whenever *fixed == PaymentCoupling::Mutator. Add regression tests for a non-tapping discounted mana ability and a cost_reduction_per tapped predicate.

Passed over: Leaving non-tapping abilities as Invariant (breaks non-tapping discounted/granted abilities); marking every ability Sensitive unconditionally (would blow up the 10-Forest {15} rejection bound).

Scope: This work (unfinished rule on a reachable surface).

On a board with a Delve spell and a graveyard-reading mana source (e.g. Condition::GraveyardAtLeast or LayerCount::Graveyard powering Raucous Audience), taking the offered Delve exile first strands the payment.
