The biggest mismatch: Academy Replay overloads CapturedCards (card-conservation telemetry) as its native-seat choice tape

To expand built-in choices when importing a native-seat record, import_record enables card-exposure capture on the verifier (walk(record, capture_cards=native_seats)) and reads replay.cards().capture.exposure.choices. That conflates two unrelated concepts: recording which (actor, action_index) pairs a verified replay committed vs. proving physical-card conservation and building card-exposure statistics for unmodified games. Two concrete counterexamples reproduced directly on HEAD (6920b1e012):

  1. Counterexample 1 — determinize on any native-seat record crashes import: Replaying a snapshot after env.determinize(99, 0) succeeds on ("agent", "agent") (stop="interrupted", count=2), because capture_cards is False. The exact same sequence on ("agent", "heuristic:64") or ("random", "heuristic") crashes import_game inside project_native_record → replay.cards() → CapturedCards.model_validate_json() with: 
    pydantic_core._pydantic_core.ValidationError: 1 validation error for CapturedCards
  Value error, card movement does not continue its physical copy
    determinize rearranges unseen hand/library copies in place, which is legal for replay (BRANCHING_INTERVENTIONS) and verified cleanly by RecordWalker, but intentionally violates CapturedCards.complete()'s physical-copy movement invariant (managym/card_capture.py:163-172).
  2. Counterexample 2 — GameStateEdit.move_permanent on a native-seat record crashes import: Editing a battlefield land to the graveyard via env.edit_game_state([GameStateEdit.move_permanent(perm_id, "graveyard")]) imports cleanly on ("agent", "agent") (stop="interrupted", count=5), but on ("agent", "heuristic:64") crashes import_game with: 
    pydantic_core._pydantic_core.ValidationError: 1 validation error for CapturedCards
  Value error, capture omitted a committed movement
    Even when no intervention is present, capture_cards=True builds CardSnapshots for every focus object of every legal option at every decision, records all CardMoves and ExposureResolutions, serializes the whole ledger to JSON, and validates every move in Pydantic just to read (actor, selected).
  3. Counterexample 3 (related Pass 7 boundary) — diverged record_stop.index disagrees with Replay's decision list: Corrupting record.games[0].digests[2] on a ("heuristic:64", "agent") record produces a Replay with count=3 verified decisions (ordinals 0, 1, 2), yet recording_import.stop.index is 1 (the external-action offset), so replay/+page.svelte:301 displays "Stopped at decision 1 · saved recording only". Corrupting end.winner produces count=37 while stop.index reports 21.

Built at 6920b1e012 (Pass 7)

  • import_record passes capture_cards=native_seats and project_native_record reads replay.cards().capture.exposure.choices, pulling in CapturedCards' physical-card conservation validator and full exposure JSON serialization.
  • determinize and GameStateEdit.move_permanent work on ("agent", "agent") imports but crash native-seat imports with ValidationError.
  • RecordingImport(stop, ...) stores external-action replay.stop.index, which disagrees with Academy Replay's expanded decision count on divergence.
  • project_recorded_choice reads decision.interventions only, shifting or dropping branched when a boundary combines branching ops with ForceAction.

Recommended contract

  • Separate committed-choice recording from card-statistics capture: add a lightweight opt-in committed-choice log (actor, selected) on Env/RecordWalker (no CardSnapshots, no CardMoves, no CapturedCards validation, rebuilt automatically on RecordWalker's cold-prefix divergence path). import_record reads committed choices directly with capture_cards=False.
  • Store replace(stop, index=len(session.canonical_decisions)) in RecordingImport so Replay's divergence banner always names the Replay decision boundary (unchanged for ("agent", "agent") records without ForceAction).
  • Carry pending BRANCHING_INTERVENTIONS across restore() and attach/clear them on the first projected choice that follows (forced, settled, or external).

Findings

What breaks: managym/academy/watch.py:490 and :651 reuse walk(record, capture_cards=True) and replay.cards().capture.exposure.choices to extract (actor, selected) for native seats. Because CapturedCards validates unmodified physical-card movement conservation, importing a native-seat record containing determinize or GameStateEdit.move_permanent crashes with ValidationError, whereas the exact same interventions import cleanly on ("agent", "agent") records. Every native-seat import also serializes and validates full card-snapshot, card-move, and exposure telemetry just to read two integers per choice.

Fix: Record committed (actor, selected) choices via a dedicated, lightweight opt-in choice log on Env / RecordWalker (recording only (actor, selected) when a nontrivial choice commits in Game::step / execute_semantic_command, with no CardSnapshot, CardMove, or CapturedCards validation, and preserved across RecordWalker's cold-prefix rebuild on divergence). Have import_record read those committed choices with capture_cards=False. Add regression tests in tests/academy/test_game_records.py importing native-seat records with determinize and GameStateEdit.move_permanent.

Passed over: Parsing raw json.loads(replay.env.card_capture_json()) without CapturedCards validation. That avoids the ValidationError with no Rust change, but keeps Academy Replay coupled to card-exposure telemetry and retains full focus-card/move serialization overhead on every native-seat import (Q1).

What breaks: managym/academy/watch.py:563-565 persists replay.stop directly into RecordingImport. When a native-seat record (or a record with ForceAction) diverges, replay.stop.index is the external-action offset, not len(session.canonical_decisions). Academy Replay's UI (academy/ui/src/routes/replay/+page.svelte:301) displays "Stopped at decision {index}", reporting e.g. decision 1 on a 3-decision Replay or decision 21 on a 37-decision Replay.

Fix: Keep external-action coordinates on RecordWalker.stop, and store replace(stop, index=len(session.canonical_decisions)) in RecordingImport in managym/academy/watch.py:563-565. Assert recording_import["stop"]["index"] == imported["count"] on diverged native-seat imports in tests/academy/test_game_records.py.

What breaks: In managym/academy/watch.py:626-634 and :682-706, project_recorded_choice reads decision.interventions only, while project_native_record's restore() executes ForceAction with decision=None. When a boundary combines a branching intervention (e.g. reseed or determinize) with ForceAction, the branched marker skips the forced choice and lands on a later external decision, or disappears entirely when ForceAction occurs at stop.index with no following external decision.

Fix: Pass explicit branched: Sequence[str] = () into project_recorded_choice. On the ("agent", "agent") path, derive it from decision.interventions as today; in project_native_record, accumulate branching intervention ops in restore() and attach/clear them on the next apply_choice(...) call (whether forced, settled, or external). Add a regression test combining reseed/determinize with ForceAction.
