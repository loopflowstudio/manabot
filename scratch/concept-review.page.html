Concrete interaction walk: normal path, failure, and recovery

Walking the coupled helped-cost interactions and boundary cases through HEAD (f3779cb5b2):

  1. Improvise / Waterbend / off-color Convoke tap before a tapped-reading mana source: Casting a {1}{G} Improvise spell with an untapped artifact and a source that makes {G} only if you control a tapped artifact. cost_obligations emits [Obligation::Helped({1}{G}, improvise)]. Pure handoff strips Mutator taps and returns None; the production loop tries the mana source first (produces {C}, fails); pay_helped queries game.tap_payments(&helped.cost, helped.permissions, source.permanent) directly on the whole remaining cost, taps the artifact via PaymentCandidate::HelpTap, shrinks helped.cost to {G}, and the child fork taps the now-enabled source for {G} and completes. Only the completing Improvise tap is offered first.
  2. Convoke before sacrificing for mana and exiling for Delve: Casting a {2}{R}{R} Convoke + Delve spell with an empty graveyard, a Mountain, and a red creature with Sacrifice this creature: Add {R}. CoupledPayment::new enters coupled mode via facts.sacrifices && help.delve.is_some() (stateful_delve() == false). Sacrificing first strands; pay_helped convokes the red creature (shrinking helped.cost to {2}{R} while keeping helped.permissions.help.delve = Some(spell) intact); the child fork sacrifices the tapped creature for {R} (putting 1 card in graveyard), taps the Mountain for {R}, and cost_after_delve reduces {2}{R} to {1}{R}, which the pool pays.
  3. Improvise before Delve removes the artifact type (failure & recovery): A Mountain that is an Artifact only while graveyard ≥ 1, 1 card in graveyard, {G}{G} in pool, casting {2}{G}{G} Delve + Improvise. delve_payment_objects probes PaymentCandidate::DelveExile(graveyard): on the post-exile fork, pure_payment_model rebuilds payment_sources from current characteristics, the Mountain is no longer an artifact (waterbend == false), and the branch fails—so exiling first is pruned. Probing PaymentCandidate::HelpTap(helper, Improvise) leaves the graveyard intact, then exiles the graveyard card and spends {G}{G}. Only the Improvise tap is offered first, then only the Delve exile, then PayCost.
  4. Nested upfront mana fee and FixedOnly boundary: When activating a mana ability with an upfront mana fee while paying a helped spell, payment_obligations_for produces [upfront_fee, Output, Obligation::Helped(spell)]; coupled.plan drains only native_mana_obligations (the upfront fee and output), keeping Obligation::Helped(spell) for the continuation after complete_upfront. Conversely, automatic_payability and automatic_taps use SourcePolicy::FixedOnly, which lowers any Obligation::Helped to plain Obligation::mana_and_life so automatic mana-only routes never alias help taps or Delve.

Pass 6 resolution: unifying helped costs into Obligation::Helped

Pass 6 moved the whole helped-cost contract onto one variant in Obligation and one native step handler in coupled.rs:

One unsplit Obligation::Helped(Box<HelpedCost>) carries remaining cost, permissions, and life in the ordered obligation list. Coupled queries emit Obligation::Helped directly; uncoupled queries keep the pure tier split in help_obligations. Delve exiles fork natively on stateful_delve() boards; otherwise cost_after_delve folds graveyard cards into the full-cost spend. Help taps query game.tap_payments against the unsplit helped.cost on every fork, keeping Convoke, Improvise, Waterbend, and Delve composable in any order.

Before Pass 6 (52fb812008)

  • CoupledPayment.delve: Option<DelveCost> tracked only Delve beside pre-flattened tap_obligations tiers recovered by suffix matching.
  • try_help_taps checked only obligations.first(), hiding generic Improvise, Waterbend, and off-color Convoke taps behind colored pips.
  • next_delve was gated on stateful_delve(), dropping Delve after a Convoke tap on sacrifice-coupled boards.
  • Partial-tier spends set saved_delve = None, stripping Delve from the generic tail.

After Pass 6 (f3779cb5b2)

  • Obligation::Helped(Box<HelpedCost>) sits directly in the ordered obligations list; no side-channel or suffix matching exists.
  • pay_helped evaluates Delve exiles, full-cost spending, and every legal Convoke / Improvise / Waterbend tap directly against the unsplit HelpedCost on each fork.
  • help_obligations lowers Obligation::Helped only for pure handoff and uncoupled queries; FixedOnly lowers it to plain mana_and_life.
  • CoupledMemoKey hashes obligations directly (including cost, waterbend, and life), and PurposeProjection reads &helped.cost.
