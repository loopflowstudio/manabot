Outcome
Replace the card-specific WaterbendOrDiscard effect/decision with reusable optional payment and continuation machinery. Generalize the existing PayOrNot and payment substrate first: typed costs/payment methods, success/decline continuations and resumable progress. Waterbending Lesson is the first real use, expressed as Draw(3), then optional Waterbend(2), continuing if paid and Discard(1) if declined. Preserve the same printed rules, legal action sequence, viewer-safe information, commitment boundaries and priority timing. Avoid an arbitrary Boolean-expression engine or a universal decision type: sequence, choice, payment and continuation are control-flow concepts; scry ordering, targeting and legend selection may remain distinct primitives.

Original problem
Before this change, WaterbendOrDiscard repeated one card's sentence across Effect, Decision, mana handling and semantic Step/opcode. The prior PayOrNot decision carried success/decline effects but only a ManaCost; the waterbend decision separately carried player, remaining and started. Both nested immediate mana choices. Announced spell/activation payments remain in PendingChoice; this change shares resolving optional payments without replacing that distinct announcement lifecycle.

Accepted design and constraints
- Separate authored rule (cost with paid/declined continuations), pending progress (payer, remaining obligation, committed payments and continuation), and complete legal offers derived from that state.
- Payment must permit the relevant mana abilities and waterbend taps through existing authorities. A tap to waterbend is not tapping for mana. Preserve restrictions/granted mana, exact source and object identities, event timing and payment feasibility; do not consume the same resource twice.
- Declining before payment and attempting to switch after partial waterbend payment are different. Preserve the existing no-partial-waterbend-then-discard contract, and preserve legitimate mana-activation side effects and committed costs. Generalization does not authorize undo/cancellation, rollback of revealed information, new priority windows, changed choice granularity or automatic action selection.
- Academy should manifest the same viewer-safe observations and complete legal action space agents experience, per Jack Heart's accepted direction. Provide reusable typed purpose/payment/continuation context where warranted; do not build a human-only payment wizard, parse card names/Oracle text in clients, or duplicate legality.
- Preserve deterministic Commands, stale/duplicate handling, replay, fork isolation, nested rollback and recovery. Source card identity must survive suspension appropriately without following a changed incarnation.
- Native and compiled authored execution must agree. Preserve frozen worlds, historical schemas/opcode assignments and evidence bytes. If old WaterbendOrDiscard opcode must remain to read old content, retain a narrow legacy lowering into the shared representation rather than a second runtime implementation. Explicitly version changed serialized rules, policy inputs and world/checkpoint identities; do not relabel old checkpoints compatible.

Concept review — 2026-10-21
The implemented model agrees with Jack Heart's accepted experience. No design change or unresolved product choice is identified. The existing authoring note already makes the interaction and recovery contract tangible; no usage-doc or skill changes are needed for this review.

| User action | Concept and state owner | Authority/API |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resolve Lesson and see three drawn cards before choosing payment | Authored sequence and exact source/targets in `EffectFrame` | `run_frame` executes Draw(3), then suspends `Decision::Payment` |
| Activate mana, choose its color, or contribute a waterbend tap | `PaymentProgress` retains the obligation and contributed objects; `ManaChoice` resumes it with the same frame | Native legal offers and ordinary Commands use mana/tap authorities; clients choose offers rather than reconstructing legality |
| Pay the remainder or decline while permitted | Exactly one outcome branch precedes the frame's remaining sequence | `execute_decision_action` consumes the payment and prepends `if_paid` or `if_declined`; decline can suspend for mandatory Discard |
| Reconnect after a lost activation response | Existing match revision, Command identity and suspended frame | Ordinary resume/duplicate handling recovers the committed color choice without repeating activation costs |

Normal path: draw, pay with mana/taps in the existing action granularity, and finish without discarding. Failure: insufficient resources leave decline available before any waterbend contribution; a tap that would strand the payment is absent from legal offers. Recovery: a lost response during chosen-color mana retains the activation costs, original frame and remaining obligation. A waterbend contribution closes decline; a mana activation alone does not. These are distinct domain commitments, not competing payment lifecycles to merge.

Counterexamples that would invalidate the model remain explicit: partial waterbend followed by discard; decline undoing Prism's activation; resumption following a source into a new incarnation; duplicate Commands executing a second continuation; or a client hiding native choices to simplify payment. Announced spell/activation payment remains distinct because its announcement ownership and lifecycle differ from a resolving optional effect. Neither a universal decision type nor a new payment service/storage lifecycle follows from the accepted requirements.

Across layers, the existing frame owns continuation context, native state owns payment progress and legal offers, Academy transports viewer-safe Commands/observations, and policy input represents both authored branches. Versioned content, state hashes and world bindings preserve the meaning of frozen evidence instead of claiming old checkpoints remain compatible. Next proof remains the configured gates and diagnostics below; this source/design review adds no test or browser certification claim.
