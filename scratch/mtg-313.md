## Implemented approach

- `managym/cards/src/nec.rs::release_to_memory` exiles once, then counts
  creatures in `Moved`. `managym/state/src/ability.rs::EffectValue::Objects
  { of, predicate }` keeps the filter on the count: `ObjectSelector::Each`
  ranges over the battlefield; `Moved` names a resolution result.
  `managym/src/flow/resolution.rs::effect_value` applies the filter; Dockside
  and discard/draw keep unfiltered counts. Serde supplies the learning tokens.
- `managym/cards/src/j22.rs` composes existing vigilance, scoped untap and
  activation. `managym/cards/src/tokens.rs::zeppelin` is a noncreature artifact
  Vehicle with printed 5/5 and existing crew animation. No input layout or
  version changes. External printing evidence is in `scratch/questions.md`.
- `managym/src/agent/ability_description.rs` keeps `selector_description(Moved)`
  as "it" and describes counts separately: "the number of cards moved this
  way" or "the number of creature cards moved this way". Concept review found
  that sharing the count phrase reworded existing flicker, unearth and untap.
  The flicker regression preserves main's "Put it onto the battlefield under
  its owner's control", not the earlier plan's "Return it" shorthand.
