Mana payment search (PaymentModel::witness)

Latest verdict (baseline, e8f7bde51 + counters): the search is still exponential on unpayable costs that pass the optimistic bounds. With N Forests, one any-colour source and a {U}{R} spell in hand, one legal-action computation expands exactly 25·4N/2+4 states: 409,604 states / 737,287 witness calls at N=14 (15 sources, 2.3–3.0 s). The reporter replay and the curated benchmarks never reach this: at most 449 and 12 calls per plan.

Harness: cargo run --release --features benchmark --example bench_payment --manifest-path managym/Cargo.toml -- boards 14 and -- replay scratch/ryan-slow-step/reporter_manabot_slow_step.py. Metric: witness calls and expanded states per workload (deterministic, identical in repeated runs); wall time is a noisy companion (other work was running on this host).
