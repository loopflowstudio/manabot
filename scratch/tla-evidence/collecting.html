Collecting Magic: The Gathering® | Avatar: The Last Airbender™: The Four Most Important Things to Know

Feature Oct 28, 2025
Max McCall Jubilee Finnegan

Harness the four elements and save the world with Magic: The Gathering® | Avatar: The Last Airbender™. We're bringing one of animation's most imaginative and emotional worlds to Magic with all the fiery flair you'd expect from this series. No matter who your favorite character is, there's something in this set for you. You can bend the battlefield alongside the Gaang when the set releases on November 21.

Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender previews have begun! We showed off a ton of new-to-Magic cards and treatments during the set's debut, and there's plenty more to come. You can read up on the details below. For now, here are the four most important things to know about collecting this set.

1. Enter the Avatar State with the Raised Foil Avatar Aang Card

0363a_MTGTLA_AangHdlr: Avatar Aang

Control the elements in style with this set's headliner card, a borderless raised foil version of Avatar Aang. Illustrated by one of the series's creators, Bryan Konietzko, this card unites the dazzling Booster Fun treatments of Magic with Avatar: The Last Airbender's style, making it a fitting capstone to your collection.

This version of Avatar Aang appears only in raised foil and only in English in any language of Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender Collector Boosters.

2. Collect Characters on Battle Pose Cards

0360_MTGTLA_IntrEcl: Fire Lord Zuko 0361_MTGTLA_IntrEcl: Katara, the Fearless

Drawing inspiration for the original series's intro sequence, battle pose cards capture Avatar: The Last Airbender's characters as they prepare to take on their foes. This set features 4 textless neon ink foil borderless battle pose cards, complete with a textless treatment to show off the artwork.

Neon ink foil battle pose cards are available only in Collector Boosters.

3. Follow the Original Series Across Source Material Cards

0013_MTGTLA_PrtnrSrc: Force of Negation 0048_MTGTLA_PrtnrSrc: Eladamri's Call

Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender's source material cards bring shots from the original series to Magic cards. There's a source material card for every episode of Avatar: The Last Airbender, for a grand total of 61 source material cards. Each source material card lists the book and episode that the artwork comes from in the bottom-left corner of the card.

Non-foil source material cards are available in Play Boosters and Collector Boosters. Traditional foil source material cards are only available in Collector Boosters.

4. Recreate Epic Showdowns on Scene Cards

0313_MTGTLA_ScneBstr: Fire Lord Azula 0315_MTGTLA_ScneBstr: Fire Lord Zuko

Relive the excitement from Book 1, Book 2, and Book 3 of Avatar: The Last Airbender with scene cards! We've recreated the climax of each book across three different collections of scene cards. Book 1 is a 4-card scene, Book 2 is a 6-card scene, and Book 3 is a massive 9-card scene that's befitting of the series finale.

Non-foil and traditional foil scene cards are available in Play Boosters and Collector Boosters.

Ready to soar into the details? Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender releases worldwide on November 21, 2025. The set is available for preorder now from your local game store, Amazon, TCGplayer, and elsewhere Magic products are sold.

Preorder Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender

0393_MTGTLA_BaBPromo: Firebending Student

Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender releases worldwide on November 21, 2025, and previews are already underway! The Booster Fun of Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender

Avatar: The Last Airbender has such a unique look and feel that we knew it deserved some stunning Booster Fun treatments. Here's a look at what you can find in your Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender products.

Borderless Raised Foil Avatar Aang

0363a_MTGTLA_AangHdlr: Avatar Aang

Harness the elements and transform Avatar Aang into Aang, Master of Elements! This stunning borderless version of Aang brings the artwork of one of the series's creators, Bryan Konietzko, to Magic for the very first time. Just as Aang fulfills his cosmic destiny to bring balance to the four nations, this raised foil treatment unifies our past Booster Fun designs with Avatar: The Last Airbender to create the ultimate collectible.

This version of Avatar Aang appears only in raised foil and only in English in any language of Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender Collector Boosters.

Borderless Battle Pose Cards

0360_MTGTLA_IntrEcl: Fire Lord Zuko

Your favorite Avatar: The Last Airbender characters are showing off their bending abilities on borderless battle pose cards. This set features 4 textless neon ink foil borderless battle pose cards, which are available only in Collector Boosters. These draw inspiration from the Avatar: The Last Airbender intro sequence, putting the spotlight on each of these characters.

0334_MTGTLA_IntrInsp: Fire Lord Azula

There are 5 additional borderless battle pose cards that can be found in Play Boosters and Collector Boosters in non-foil and traditional foil. These do not appear in neon ink foil and are not textless.

Borderless Source Material Cards

0016_MTGTLA_PrtnrSrc: Mystic Remora 0056_MTGTLA_PrtnrSrc: Dark Depths

We're bringing your favorite episodes of Avatar: The Last Airbender to Magic cards! Source material cards combine shots from the original series with thematic Magic reprints. Some of these cards even feature alternate names for maximum flavor, like Dark Depths as "The Boy in the Iceberg."

Each source material card lists the book and episode that the artwork comes from in the bottom-left corner of the card. There are 61 source material cards, with one for each episode of the original series. It's a great excuse to go back and rewatch episodes of Avatar: The Last Airbender.

A non-foil source material card appears in 1 of every 26 Play Boosters. A non-foil or traditional foil source material card appears in every Collector Booster.

Borderless Scene Cards

0313_MTGTLA_ScneBstr: Fire Lord Azula 0315_MTGTLA_ScneBstr: Fire Lord Zuko

We've brought the epic showdowns of each book's finale to life on three sets of scene cards, available in Play Boosters and Collector Boosters. By placing these cards side by side, you can see these world-shaking moments through the lens of Magic. Book 1 is a 4-card scene, Book 2 is a 6-card scene, and Book 3 is a massive 9-card scene that's befitting of the series finale.

Book 1, Book 2, and Book 3 scene cards appear in non-foil and traditional foil in Play Boosters and Collector Boosters.

0063_MTGTLA_SceneNew: Katara's Reversal 0071_MTGTLA_SceneNew: Sokka and Suki

This set also has two Scene Boxes—Tea Time at the Jasmine Dragon and The Black Sun Invasion—that each have their own 6-card scene made up of traditional foil new-to-Magic cards. These cards also appear in non-foil in Collector Boosters.

Borderless Double-Faced Sagas

0355a_MTGTLA_BdlsSaga: The Legend of Kuruk 0356a_MTGTLA_BdlsSaga: The Rise of Sozin

By the time we meet Aang in the original series, the prior Avatars and their foes are mythical figures. We've depicted their stories on a cycle of mythic rare borderless Sagas. Each Saga depicts a former Avatar or Sozin, tying them directly to the lore and worldbuilding of the Avatar: The Last Airbender universe.

There are 5 mythic rare borderless double-faced Sagas in this set. Borderless double-faced Sagas appear in non-foil and traditional foil in Play Boosters and Collector Boosters.

Borderless Field Notes

0325_MTGTLA_FldNotes: Ran and Shaw 0326_MTGTLA_FldNotes: Badgermole Cub

The world of Avatar: The Last Airbender is full of friendly (and sometimes frightening) creatures. We've depicted some of our favorite creatures and creations on borderless field notes cards. Hybrid animals like the Badgermole Cub are an especially cute version of this treatment. But some cards, like the firebending masters Ran and Shaw, lean more toward the epic side of the series.

There are 8 rare and 7 mythic rare field notes cards. Field notes cards appear in non-foil and traditional foil in Play Boosters and Collector Boosters.

Elemental Frame Cards

0343_MTGTLA_ShowElem: Redirect Lightning 0351_MTGTLA_ShowElem: Katara, Water Tribe's Hope

Combining the in-universe aesthetics of the Avatar: The Last Airbender series with the Magic card frame, elemental frame cards depict your favorite characters and moments in Booster Fun style. With different etchings in the text-box frame depending on the element being depicted, this treatment complements the artwork of the series beautifully.

There are 15 rare and 5 mythic rare elemental frame cards. Elemental frame cards appear in non-foil and traditional foil in Play Boosters and Collector Boosters.

Full-Art Avatar's Journey Lands

0292_MTGTLA_MBLnds: Plains 0293_MTGTLA_MBLnds: Island 0294_MTGTLA_MBLnds: Swamp 0295_MTGTLA_MBLnds: Mountain 0296_MTGTLA_MBLnds: Forest

Aang's journey took him across the four nations of the Avatar: The Last Airbender world. You can follow in his footsteps with these Avatar's journey lands! Each of these lands features a setting from the original series.

Full-art Avatar's journey lands can be found in Play Boosters and Collector Boosters in non-foil and traditional foil.

Full-Art Appa Lands

0287_MTGTLA_AppaLand: Plains 0288_MTGTLA_AppaLand: Island 0289_MTGTLA_AppaLand: Swamp 0290_MTGTLA_AppaLand: Mountain 0291_MTGTLA_AppaLand: Forest

Yip yip! Take flight alongside Appa on this series of full-art Appa basic lands. Depicting various moments during Aang's journey, these lands follow Appa's path across the skies of Avatar: The Last Airbender's world. Can you name the moments from the original series that each card depicts?

Full-art Appa lands can be found in Play Boosters, Collector Boosters, Bundles, Commander Bundles, and Jumpstart Boosters in non-foil and traditional foil.

Collector Boosters

Each Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender Collector Booster box contains 12 Collector Boosters, each of which contains the following:

  • 15 Magic: The Gathering cards
    • 3 Traditional foil commons
      • There are 81 commons and 10 common dual lands from Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender that can be found in these slots.
    • 3 Traditional foil uncommons
      • There are 110 uncommons from the main set of Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender that can be found in these slots.
    • 2 Traditional foil TLE commons
      • There are 22 commons from the Jumpstart product that can be found in these slots.
      • There are 31 commons from the Beginner Box that can be found in these slots.
    • 1 Traditional foil TLE or Booster Fun uncommon
      • There are 4 scene cards (8%) from the main set of Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender that can be found in these slots.
      • There are 32 uncommons from the Jumpstart product that can be found in these slots.
      • There are 14 uncommons from the Beginner Box that can be found in these slots.
    • 1 Traditional foil full-art Appa (50%) or Avatar's journey (50%) basic land
    • 1 Traditional foil rare or mythic rare
      • There are 60 rares (85.7%) and 20 mythic rares (14.3%) from the main set of Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender that can be found in these slots.
    • 1 Traditional foil TLE rare or mythic rare
      • There are 32 rares (37%) and 11 mythic rares (6.4%) from the Jumpstart product that can be found in these slots.
      • There are 10 rares (11.5%) from the Beginner Box that can be found in these slots.
      • Extended-art versions of 28 rares (32.4%) and 11 mythic rares (12.7%) from the Jumpstart product can be found in these slots.
    • 1 Non-foil Booster Fun rare or mythic rare
      • A rare (7.8%) or mythic rare (1.1%) scene card
      • A rare (6.9%) or mythic rare (3%) field notes card
      • A rare (1.3%) or mythic rare (less than 1%) battle pose card
      • A rare (11.7%) or mythic rare (1.1%) elemental frame card
      • A mythic rare borderless double-faced Saga card (2.2%)
      • An extended-art version of 28 rares (24.2%) or 1 mythic rare (less than 1%) version of a card from the main set
      • An extended-art rare (24.2%) or mythic rare (4.8%) version of a card from the Jumpstart product
      • A rare new-to-Magic card (10.4%) from the Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender Scene Boxes
    • 1 Non-foil (75%) or traditional foil (25%) source material card
    • 1 Foil Booster Fun rare or mythic rare
      • A rare (12.8%) or mythic rare (1.8%) scene card
      • A rare (11.4%) or mythic rare (5%) field notes card
      • A traditional foil rare (2.1%) or mythic rare (1.4%) battle pose card

  • 1 Art card (15%) or traditional foil double-sided token (85%)
    • Of the art cards, 3% are gold stamped with an artist's signature or the Planeswalker symbol.

Note: Percentages may not sum to 100 due to rounding.

Jumpstart Boosters

Each Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender Jumpstart Booster box contains 24 Jumpstart Boosters, each of which contains the following:

  • 20 Magic: The Gathering cards
    • 46 Possible Jumpstart themes, some of which have multiple variants
    • 1–2 Rare or mythic rare cards
    • Includes lands
  • 1 Theme insert

Bundles

0394_MTGTLA_BdlPromo: Momo, Friendly Flier

Each Bundle includes the following:

  • 9 Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender Play Boosters
  • 15 Traditional foil basic lands
    • Includes 5 full-art Appa lands
  • 15 Non-foil basic lands
    • Includes 5 full-art Appa lands
  • 2 Reference cards
  • 1 Traditional foil promo card
  • 1 Oversized spindown life counter

Commander's Bundles

Score Commander staples and explore the newest set! Each Commander's Bundle contains the following:

  • 9 Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender Play Boosters
  • 1 Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender Collector Booster
  • 5 Non-foil promo cards
    • Three (3) of these promo cards are the same in each Commander's Bundle.
  • 15 Traditional foil basic lands
    • Includes 5 full-art Appa lands
  • 15 Non-foil basic lands
    • Includes 5 full-art Appa lands
  • 1 Click-wheel

Please note that the Commander's Bundle only contains Appa basic lands and does not include Avatar's journey basic lands as described on the packaging. Boosters found inside the product can still contain Avatar's journey basic lands.

Scene Boxes

Each Scene Box includes the following:

  • 3 Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender Play Boosters
  • 6 Traditional foil borderless scene cards
  • 6 Art cards
  • 1 Display Easel

Beginner Boxes

Learn to play Magic or teach your friends! Each Beginner Box contains the following:

  • 10 Themed Jumpstart half-decks
    • Two (2) of these Jumpstart half-decks are tutorial decks and are designed to be played against each other.
    • Each half-deck includes 20 cards. Shuffle two together and play!
  • 5 Non-foil tokens
  • 2 Gameboard playmats
  • 2 "How to Play" guides
  • 2 Reference cards
  • 2 Spindown dice
  • 1 Reference guide booklet

Prerelease Packs

Your seeded Character Booster may appear in clear cellophane depending on where it was printed.

Choose one of five seeded Prerelease Packs and begin your journey with Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender. Each Prerelease Pack contains the following:

  • 5 Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender Play Boosters
  • 1 Seeded Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender Character Booster
    • Each Character Booster is themed around a different Avatar: The Last Airbender character and a color of Magic.
  • 2 Non-foil double-sided tokens themed to your Prerelease Pack's color
  • 1 Traditional foil year-stamped rare or mythic rare
  • 1 Spindown die themed to your Prerelease Pack's color
