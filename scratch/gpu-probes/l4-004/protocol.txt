L4 learner batch-shape and repeatability validation — preregistration

Purpose: replace unmeasured learner scaling assumptions with actual fixed-shape
service and distinguish ordinary repeatability noise from the catalog cache's
small Adam-state difference. This is a bounded performance/correctness probe,
not a strength campaign or production optimization.

Reuse the existing ordinary cuda_performance TrainingRun owner. Width64/depth1,
float32/TF32off, Ataraxos move recipe, current-self, fixed initialization10349,
three learning iterations per child. Fresh process for every case. All sizes are
complete centrally filtered batches; do not simulate summing independent Adam
owners. Shape is [learner timesteps, streams].

Cases, in order:
- Four [8,64] cases: reference-A, cached-A, reference-B, cached-B. Compare raw,
  EMA and Adam states within reference/reference and cached/cached, then across
  variants. Preserve every non-exact tensor and absolute differences; no new

  the control; do not claim all future schedules deterministic from one machine.
- [8,512] reference/cached, 4096 learner rows per iteration.
- [8,4096] reference/cached, 32768 learner rows per iteration.

The research cache is restricted to one no-gradient frozen-policy collection and
restored before updates. No production rule or optimizer changes. Record
synchronized collect/update-iteration phase times individually, preserving their
order; update timing excludes EMA, while TrainingRun's complete learning time
includes it. Instrumentation is identical in all cases. Keep ordinary stage and
run times, actual exposures/optimizer steps, skips/nonfinite reports, world/source
identities and exported raw/EMA/Adam artifacts. Larger batches change the filter
population; compare shapes as configurations, not identical learning workloads.

Predictions: reference-B versus reference-A can reproduce tiny Adam differences
without caching (alternative: discrepancy only appears across cache variants).
