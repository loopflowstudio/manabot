concepts are the ones the brief asked for: one campaign protocol naming one
opponent (checkpoint or `heuristic`), a fixed slice of the frozen shuffle, the
v3 failure policy and the inclusive ledger. Checked and deliberately kept:

  leave it for work that already changes the heuristic.

  as a "pilot" and projects from them; harmless here (report uses neither).
  Pooling across campaigns lives in the published `summarize.py`, not the
  driver. If continuations recur, make "one cohort, several campaigns" a
  driver concept then — not needed for this Task.
