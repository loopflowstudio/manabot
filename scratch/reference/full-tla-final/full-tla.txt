**Research handoff — 2026-10-28.** Jack Heart accepted full TLA support,
any valid user-supplied TLA deck through the shared human/bot engine, decklist-file
input and the seven-TLA-plus-one-import delivery plan. Automatic implementation
and publication are authorized; every merge requires his review. At most two or
three card groups run concurrently. Detailed mechanisms and per-card allocation
below are implementation planning, not a fresh approval gate.

The parent operator launched **892c47** (T1 content authority) and **f871a8**
(D1 file import) through `code` to human demo review. Preserve those Task identities;
this research Graph launches no duplicates and makes no production changes. Scope
and accepted provenance: [tla-scope.md](tla-scope.md), [delivery record](tla-delivery-batches.md).

The intended win is that a person and a manabot can play every TLA card through
one rules engine, understand every available decision, and replay or fork it
without changing its meaning. Completion means correct behavior, not registration
of all names. Current coverage is an authored-deck subset; expanding that subset
without fixing its underlying abstractions would multiply special cases.

## What the investigation establishes

**76/286 cards have native definitions; 74 occur in an embedded compiled catalog;
69 occur in current `elemental-world-v2`.** All 76 are classified **partial**, not
fully supported. The remaining 210 cards (218 face rows) have no native definition.
No unreviewed card has been promoted on the basis of its printed text or matching
name. Per-face obligation summaries were read against the retrieved card text;
family tags are conservative triage aids, not a proof that every family gap affects
every tagged card. Test references include fixture mentions and must be read as
bounded witnesses, not clause-by-clause certification.

The current code is **w14/hash9**. Native `ContentPack` definitions and compiled
semantic programs coexist. `SemanticPack::compile_content_pack` lowers typed
programs into the same `CardDefinition`/`Effect` engine, but constructs
`ContentPack::default()` just to copy presentation text. This makes a native
registration an unnecessary prerequisite for a new compiled card. Exact setup
matching selects compiled packs; arbitrary deck edits cannot use that admission.
The existing differential definition test establishes coherence, not correctness.

## Chosen architecture and deletion path

Use reviewed typed content lowered into **one native rules runtime**. Card text
is presentation/source evidence, never a runtime parser. A card admission is a
versioned manifest assertion that every face and related object has executable
programs and independent expected behavior witnesses. Missing programs, partial
faces, unknown opcodes and stale manifests reject admission explicitly.

1. **Content authority, first deletion cut.** Separate immutable presentation data
   from `ContentPack::default()` in `compile_content_pack`. Make current TLA typed
   authoring the sole rule definition; generate the native pack and learning
   projection from its IR. Move the two native-only cards into that source with
   their existing behavior tests. Setup manifests reference eligible definitions
   independently of runtime semantics. Preserve historical authored setups as
   presets; new pool/format admission must accept any valid submitted combination.
   The research ledger is evidence, never the runtime admission database.
2. **Exact object/stack/permission state.** Extend existing `ObjectRef`, stack
   objects and resolution frames rather than adding an airbend side engine.
   Casting grants identify destination incarnation, eligible actor, origin zone,
   alternative cost, duration and timing modifiers. The shared cast planner
   composes grants with prohibitions, additional costs and the existing
   `PaymentModel`; execution validates the same typed plan. Keep distinct checks
   when announcing, accepting a Command and beginning resolution. Retained
   eligibility governs instructions within one resolution.
3. **One effective-characteristic view.** Put layer, timestamp/dependency,
   controller, subtype/name, ability and P/T evaluation behind native queries.
   Use those queries for predicates, payment abilities, trigger matching,
   combat, LKI and viewer-safe observations. Copying reads copiable values, not
   the final layered view. Printed definitions remain immutable.
4. **Resumable event and decision state.** Extend existing event commit,
   trigger placement and continuation machinery. Replacement choice keeps the
   proposed event and applied-effect set; each choice recomputes applicability.
   Modes, reflexive targets, distributions, copied stack objects and control-player
   decisions are authoritative pending decisions, hashed and journaled. No UI
   selection is a substitute for a required shared choice.

**Delete—do not maintain:** remove current executable TLA registrations from
`cardsets/tla.rs` and TLA branches of `elemental.rs`, `sacrifice.rs`, `temur.rs`
after the content cutover, retaining non-TLA rules and shared token definitions
where still needed. Remove the presentation-only native-registry dependency in
`compile_content_pack`; replace current TLA native-versus-compiled equality as the
admission oracle with independent rule outcomes, preserving all behavioral tests.
Replace fixed source-order replacement selection in `collect_replacements` when
the ordered-choice implementation lands. Replace bare-card earthbend destination
lookup and migrate special death/flashback replacements into the event authority
when their equivalent independent cases are ready. MTG-177 owns deletion of
redundant per-instruction target checks; do not modernize that obsolete path first.

Frozen content/IR/worlds, checkpoint receipts and old conformance corpora remain
unchanged and addressable by their original IDs. Historical readers may read
immutable old schemas; they must not become an alternate current-world executor.
A new typed source may generate several setup manifests, not several copies of
current rules. No Legacy/New runtime split, dual writes, hand-coded browser rules,
name-dispatched exceptions or silent fallback to the generic registry is accepted.

## Decklist input contract (D1)

`manabot.env.match.parse_deck` currently parses JSON or comma-separated `name:count`
strings, with only test call sites found. It is not a file validator. Academy's
`_parse_game_config`, `CuratedPack.player_config` and native `select_authored_runtime`
couple named presets to compiled setup admission; accepting a custom dictionary
alone does not establish the required compiled-pool path.

Propose one UTF-8 counted-line dialect: optional `Deck`/`Sideboard` sections,
`4 Card Name`, blank lines and whole-line `#` comments. Resolve against the selected
pool by canonical front/full DFC name; reject reverse-face-only, unknown, ambiguous,
unsupported and nonpositive/malformed entries. Merge repeated rows while retaining
all line references; suggestions never silently substitute. Keep original bytes,
digest and editable text separately from normalized counts and resolved identities.
Unreadable/invalid UTF-8/empty files and all validation failures leave the original
source available and start no partial game.

One manabot parser is imported by Academy. A native `admit_setup(pool, format, decks)`
returns a typed admitted setup or structured issues after validating pool closure,
counts and explicit construction policy. The named product format remains to select;
initial proof uses a clearly labeled development ruleset based on existing repository
setup constraints, not an invented claim of Standard legality. Syntax and format
validation remain separate. No browser-only rules or runtime network lookup.

Persist pool/format/world/setup identity in roots and records. CLI/configuration,
Academy file control and new-game wire input, training/eval, replay and checkpoint
admission consume that setup. Old exact-roster checkpoints remain restricted; a file
cannot make them compatible. D1 does not alter PR383's curriculum choices. Private
source/sideboard information must not enter public replay. Preserve historical named
presets and replace their role as the only current pool admission route.

Demo: import a novel supported deck with one typo, see the exact line and retained
text, correct it, and start the identical compiled setup in Academy and manabot.
Proposed headless proof: `uv run --extra dev python -m scripts.verify_decklist_import`.
It tests both entry paths, duplicate rows, DFC identity, invalid counts/format,
unsupported face rejection, preserved source, equal setup digests and replay.
D1 owns a complete playable flow, not just a parser. Remove unused `parse_deck` and
its exclusive tests if production call-site inspection still confirms absence;
cut current custom-deck normalization over to the admitted result, retaining old
record readers. No second parser/admission implementation or generic-pack fallback.

## Admission, compatibility and acceptance

The full end state admits any valid user-supplied TLA deck, including decklist files,
under a named two-player format through the shared native admission boundary.
Development fixtures are intermediate proof inputs, not the final admission boundary.
Specific product format rules remain an explicit selection; D1 isolates that policy.
Do not infer retail Draft/Sealed, Commander, multiplayer, new product decks or
training strength from card completeness. Full cross-set MTG conformance is excluded;
planeswalkers/battles are not in this TLA file. Any-target universality outside the
admitted world remains separately documented, not silently claimed.

Each behavior change gets a new development world identity and content digest.
State/hash, Command/capture schema, observation fields and learning vocabulary are
versioned when their contracts change. New code chooses the next available versions
at implementation time; w15 is already reserved in MTG-177. Preserve absent-version
v1 checkpoint semantics via `ObservationConfig.from_checkpoint`; no shape-compatible
checkpoint is silently upgraded. Use the ordinary catalog/reload and arena-admission
checks. Coordinate vector buffer changes with PR383's owner, without selecting its
curriculum decisions.

Uncapped choices mean every finite legal choice in a represented state remains
reachable. Extend compound/ragged support across new ordered, variable and numeric
choices; do not enumerate exponential subsets in a flat action list. Test controlled
65-candidate and three-focus fixtures, ordered-choice prefixes and dynamic object
counts. Legacy encoders retain explicit capacity rejection and cannot be advertised
as full-TLA training. A process resource limit is an explicit failed/aborted episode,
never an altered rule or hidden action cap. Measure actual inference/memory after
schemas settle; no unsupported latency/throughput claim is part of this draft.

Gate for each implementation chunk runs its focused independent native witnesses
plus the shared Command/reconnect/replay proof **once**, then the required change-area
checks in `docs/development/validation.md`: debug Rust with two Cargo jobs, format/
Clippy, current-world admission/model/monitor checks, learning projection and focused
Academy protocol/browser cases. Complete generation/check/build before browser servers;
all server subprocesses are reaped. No random search, stress workload, paid compute or
strength campaign is needed for these decisions. Missing native/browser tooling is
reported and assigned to capable CI, never marked passed.

The full file is done when valid novel decks imported from files are admitted for
both Academy and manabot, malformed/unsupported input returns actionable issues
without losing source, and every required face/token has clause-level independent
expected results and positive/negative interaction witnesses; compiled admission
rejects a missing face/token/program; both consumers can express every legal choice
with viewer-safe information; reconnect retains actual pending decisions; recorded
Commands and isolated forks reproduce canonical consequences; incompatible historical
checkpoints are rejected while historical evidence remains readable. The ledger's
inventory completeness alone satisfies none of those behavioral conditions.

## Alternatives, success and failure

A native-registration-only expansion is initially cheap but preserves duplicate
semantic authoring and cannot produce complete learning programs. A second engine
behind an adapter moves the same hard problems into observation/action/state translation
and replay identity; no compatible drop-in has been established. Runtime Oracle-text
interpretation adds an unbounded interpretation problem and loses deterministic,
reviewable programs. None is selected. Expand the existing typed kernel and delete
current duplication.

Wild success: a newly authored card mostly composes existing operations; a designer
can inspect the same strategic decision a policy sees, and the first surprising
interaction becomes a small independent witness. Track the proportion of admissions
requiring kernel edits, but do not mistake that ratio for rule fidelity.

Wild failure: hundreds of definitions turn green against their own generated tests,
fixed buffers exclude choices, and per-card flags fight over identity/layers. The
architecture prevents that through independent outcomes, one effective view, explicit
pending decisions and deletion of duplicate current rules.

**Check result — 2026-10-28:** offline inventory/face/printing/closure verification
passed; 76 fixture comparisons had zero drift; 201 focused debug rules tests and one
100-definition compiled/native parity test passed. The rules suites include a retained
fixed-deck replay case; this run added no random search. No full Rust suite, browser
proof, policy training or whole-card conformance was claimed. Raw logs and the exact
command receipt are in `tla-evidence/validation.json`.

Remaining design details and evidence limits are in [questions.md](questions.md). Production behavior,
authored decks, Task identity and historical evidence were not changed.
