## Implemented owners and treatment

- `manabot/llm/openrouter.py`: `OpenRouterConfig.transport` selects existing chat
  or typed Decisions in the same session. `DecisionRequest` sends full state and
  one win-game question. Every legal reference has its native renderer description;
  the adapter rejects missing descriptions, more than 255 choices and payload
  overflow before dispatch. Typed replies retain answers, model/provider, native
  input/output counts and cost without fabricated completions. No Jev repair,
  random fallback, probability override or automatic provider retry exists.
- `DecisionInput.action_descriptions` is optional for old text receipts and
  required for typed choices. `verify/llm_game.py` obtains it from the same capture
  used for Command binding, recognizes `openrouter-decisions-v1`, and accounts for
  typed usage. Native capture, stale rejection, execution and replay retain their
  existing owner. Historical report imports remain independent of executors.
- `manabot/llm/budget.py`: SQLite FULL-synchronous transactions reserve a supplied,
  justified inclusive maximum before every request, including chat repairs when
  a ledger is supplied. Charges round upward to nanodollars; the spending ceiling
  rounds downward so admission cannot exceed a fractional ceiling. Unpriced, malformed,
  failed or interrupted requests keep reservations and stop further admission.
  A charge above its bound is retained and blocks restart. Non-API costs reserve
  their full allowance up front; configuration rejects an allowance above the
  inclusive ceiling before ledger creation. The ledger validates supplied amounts; it cannot
  establish the truth of a provider billing guarantee from an arbitrary number.
- `experiments/jev_mtg183.py`: one frozen protocol, one sequential campaign,
  durable game admissions, no replacement games or automatic continuation.
  Protocol pins checkpoint, ratings/selection evidence, billing evidence,
  source/native/driver identity and the inclusive budget. Ordinary checkpoint
  world/setup admission remains mandatory. Reports expose all attempted and
  unattempted games and score only completed, replay-matching, failure-free games.
- `configs/llm/openrouter-jev.json` selects `typesafe/jev-1.14`, typed Decisions,
  a winning objective, 120-second request deadline and 262,144-byte payload limit.
  Chat reasoning/output/fallback fields do not control Decisions.

The engineering treatment defaults to 20 pilot games included in the same cohort
and budget, 32 public history rows, forced-choice skipping for both players,
stochastic CPU policy inference, 2,000 Commands and 3,600 seconds per game, and a
24-hour campaign allowance. The 1,000-game schedule has five independent deal
seeds for each of 100 ordered matchups and both Jev seats. A frozen shuffle makes
the affordable prefix usable without selecting games by outcomes. Report actual
seat populations; the prefix may be unbalanced. No paired-deal claim is made.
Wilson intervals describe win frequency among valid games (draws are non-wins),
not fixed-sample coverage after cost-based stopping. Only a complete, valid pilot
produces the 1,000-game projection. These are recorded engineering choices, not
new statistical decisions attributed to Jack Heart.
