# Elemental: ten proposed training decks

Accepted deck selection by Jack Heart in MTG-157, 2026-10-20. Balance remains unvalidated. Each main deck has 40 cards. All nonbasic names come from the Elemental cube. Cube quantities apply independently to each deck, counting its main deck and Lesson pool together; overlap between decks is allowed. Basic lands are unlimited. The three- or four-card Lesson pools are outside the main deck and are available only through legal effects such as Learn; they are not automatic access or between-game swaps.

Source: https://cubecobra.com/cube/download/csv/elemental, retrieved 2026-10-20 (540 cards, 399 names). The CSV snapshot is retained alongside this proposal. These lists supersede the earlier deck cores and shared-inventory assumption.

These are the accepted complete starting lists, not balance-tested or fully implemented claims. They preserve the ten requested strategic identities. The available cube commons constrain creature density, especially in GW and UG; those decks need more uncommons. Iroh and Ran and Shaw are explicit selections for UG, now Temur Lessons. Implementation status is tracked in elemental-design.md; new-world publication and final ten-deck admission remain unfinished.

## Validation and remaining work

- Validated all ten main-deck totals, per-deck main-plus-pool quantities against the cube, the four-copy ceiling for nonbasics, and the Lesson subtype of every outside-game pool card.
- All decks have 17 lands except UG, which has 18. Fetch lands can overlap between decks. Mana bases are preliminary; no mulligan or match simulation has been run.
- Similar strength is a design target, not a measured result. First balance checks should scrutinize GW Ally snowballing, UR Swarm consistency, BG landfall resource generation, and UG surviving long enough to use its top end.
- The lists intentionally introduce no Adventures, transforming Sagas, planeswalkers, or copied-ability creatures. They still require a card-by-card support audit before implementation.
- Material support checks include hybrid payments; selected sacrifice costs and sacrifice-trigger context; landfall and fetch-land searching; Food and Treasure; prowess; Spirit evasion; mana restrictions and grants; Iroh flashback and timing; Ran and Shaw copying and its cast/graveyard conditions; and legend handling. Existing registration is not proof of full authored-runtime support.
- Do not replace printed rules with reduced mechanics to fit the engine. Preserve the existing Allies/Lessons world and frozen evidence; any implementation belongs to a new versioned world.
