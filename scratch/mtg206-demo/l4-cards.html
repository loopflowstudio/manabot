GIH WR
Win rate over copies in the opening hand or drawn later. Each copy counts, so two copies in hand count twice. Tutored or returned copies count only if later drawn. The interval resamples whole deals across every run.
OH, GD
The same, split into opening-hand copies and copies drawn after the opening hand.
GP
Games with the card in the deck. GNS: those games in which no copy reached the hand.
IWD
GIH WR minus GNS WR, in percentage points.
Games
Player-games with a winner and a passed replay. With all subjects, each game counts once for each side, so win rates center on 50%; pick a subject to see one player's cards. Draws, crashes, timeouts and failed replays are listed separately and never earn a win. These are associations between cards and wins, not proof that a card causes them.
