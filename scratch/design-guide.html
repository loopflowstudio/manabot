The proposal totals 36,000 scientific games: nine initialization cohorts plus nine each of trained midpoint, trained endpoint, frozen midpoint and frozen endpoint. Calibration is additional.

What would count as useful?

Positive greedy improvement in all three seeds, at least a 10-point mean gain, and a seed-level lower interval above zero, plus no demonstrated random-anchor regression. Three seeds remain a small sample; this is not product-baseline promotion.

Report seed uncertainty separately from deal-level evaluation noise. Plot transitions, optimizer exposures, active training time and inclusive cost. Thresholds not reached remain censored; two later milestones cannot locate an exact time to progress.

Design detail · Analysis and reporting

Local design draft · refreshed 13 October 2026

Before running, proposed prediction: L1 gains at least 10 percentage points in mean greedy score over frozen initialization by N, positive in all three seeds; L2 gains at least 5 points; L3 gains less than L1. These are design predictions, not evidence or historical diagnoses. The endpoint is the primary contrast; midpoint and time-to-progress are descriptive, not sequential significance tests.

Per pool/anchor/seed/cell report wins/draws/losses and score = win + half draw, paired change versus frozen control, and uncertainty. Pool summaries equally weight four cells then seeds, never pool games as independent training seeds. Show all three paired seed effects, their mean and a 95% t interval (df=2) explicitly labeled small-sample/assumption-sensitive. Separately show paired whole-deal bootstrap intervals conditional on each checkpoint, using 10,000 replicates and fixed analysis seed 12290. Cross-pool contrasts pair training seeds but never pretend different deck worlds share identical deals.

A useful positive diagnostic requires complete legal/replayed cohorts, positive greedy improvement in all three seeds, mean improvement ≥10 points and a seed-level lower interval above zero, plus no demonstrated random-anchor regression (report its interval rather than treating a nonsignificant difference as equivalence). This is a conservative descriptive gate, not a multiplicity- corrected method-selection test. Do not search many card lists for a winner. Report each L2/L3 contrast even if the simple result fails this criterion.

Time to declared progress is the first observed midpoint/endpoint meeting the 10-point greedy improvement threshold for that seed, confirmed at endpoint. Report interval censoring between observed checkpoints; not reached is right- censored, never zero time or an extrapolated failure probability. No adaptive extra milestone. Freeze cost cutoffs from calibration; at each cutoff use the last complete checkpoint available, only over overlapping observed ranges. No interpolation or invented initialization/history.

Show three axes: actual learner decisions/transitions and optimizer exposures; active collection/update seconds; inclusive elapsed cost (setup, exports, evaluation/replay, transfers and failures). Charge shared initialization/control and calibration once in campaign accounting and explicitly allocate shared measurement overhead when showing per-arm costs. Sum parallel process cost separately from occupied wall time. Fixed controls' training exposure stays zero. Do not plot them as if they consumed the learner's samples.

The notebook leads with status/freshness, this within-pool improvement and all four role cells, then learning diagnostics and confounds. Trace every point to run/checkpoint/protocol/arena hashes. Explain greedy baseline asymmetry, action mix, land/curve differences, horizons, filtered exposure and inference latency. Anchor-vs-anchor calibration is context, not a correction factor that makes raw scores comparable. Policy entropy is not competence. Neither a simple success nor a full-world point estimate promotes the product baseline (ETU-118 owns that).

Extend docs/experiment-metrics.md for formulas, clocks, denominators, censoring and unavailable states. Use existing create-once notebook/report regeneration, optional W&B projections and private content-hashed S3 publication/readback. TrainingRun/VerifyStore and immutable arena evidence remain authority. Missing credentials mean publication unavailable, not fabricated archival; keep local originals. Never publish hidden worlds/private replay targets into viewer reports.

Design detail · What the existing system actually supports

Local design draft · refreshed 13 October 2026

  • Experiment.resolve() and its Environment, Model, Pipeline and RunControl components already own declarative recipes and provenance. TrainingRegime, ExperimentSchedule, run_experiment, TrainingRun and VerifyStore own execution and persistence. Reuse them; no new experiment DB, worker scheduler, checkpoint format or tracker authority.
  • managym/src/semantic/content.rs::AuthoredRuntime::matches requires the exact multiset of authored deck and sideboard pairs. A subset or same-deck mirror does not select the authored compiled pack. flow/setup.rs then uses general content. This is a real semantic/identity change, not just a smaller deck.
  • SemanticCardEncoder (manabot/model/semantic_cards.py) accepts only ur-lessons-vs-gw-allies and constructs its transport-ID lookup from that exact authored matchup. BoundSemanticPack.bind can join another manifest by checked registry names, but the ordinary encoder does not use the runtime subset manifest. Reusing its integer IDs on a general-pack subset is unsafe. Its token vocabulary is also catalog-dependent: independently shrinking catalogs would vary both representation and parameter count.
  • checkpoint_world/validate_checkpoint_world bind exact setup pairs, actual content, rules and input schema. validate_agent_setup rejects another pair, including mirrors of a trained cross pair. This is deliberate admission, not an obstacle to remove with a bypass.
  • monitor_evaluation._manifest explicitly requires selected_match(). arena.match.play_cell supports the selected cross matchup or historical Interactive mirror, not arbitrary pools or the four deck-role matrix. MonitorProtocol has one anchor, four-leg summaries; the study protocol has a closed discriminator. New science must not masquerade as an existing study. ETU-125's requested matrix is the behavioral contract to reuse; its integrated implementation is not present in this base. Adopt it when available rather than creating a competing evaluation path.
  • SeatRoutedCollector initializes every stream with the same ordered match and alternates only the learner seat. That balances learner seats, not each deck across play order. Its collect(num_steps) takes that many transitions per stream. Balanced deck assignments need explicit collector support; neither balance nor batch size can be inferred from four streams alone.
  • teacher1_evidence.runtime_fingerprints takes explicit match and observation arguments, but defaults to Interactive. Every pool/cell must pass actual arguments. Never rewrite frozen fingerprints to make them fit.
  • GameSession, Trace, _persist_attempt, and the table's experience_protocol.py already own player execution and durable attempt records, with private canonical replay stored in AttemptStore. Canonical historical decisions are not every automatic pass or engine microstep. The experiment uses arena command journals/replay, not table decision counts as a learning clock and not a new session/identity store. This design proves neither policy-free recovery nor crash durability of arbitrary execution.
  • The selected-world checkpoint loader is sim.flat_mc.load_checkpoint_agent; it validates the world before loading weights and checks compiled semantic input. Synthetic fixtures can test that path, but cannot admit a real trained candidate or establish completed human games.

Design detail · Evaluation, cost and admission protocol

Local design draft · refreshed 13 October 2026

Matrix and pairing

For each pool/checkpoint/anchor, score four cells independently: model A vs anchor A, A vs B, B vs A, B vs B. A/B mean swift/heavy in L1/L2, and GW Allies/UR Lessons in L3. Each cell has 50 held-out deal blocks × two play orders = 100 games. Both anchors receive this cohort: 800 games per checkpoint. Candidate and frozen control share exact within-pool assignments, deal seeds and policy/anchor random streams. Deal blocks keep both play orders and all four cells together in uncertainty calculations. Training gets disjoint seed namespaces; exported actual seeds are checked for collision, not assumed disjoint merely because namespace labels differ.

Reserve separate disjoint 50-deal families for initialization, midpoint and endpoint. Freeze all before scoring; endpoint is not inspected to select a checkpoint. Controls evaluated on each new family supply that family's exact initialization baseline. Consequently each milestone's learning contrast is trained minus its same-deal frozen initialization, not subtraction of two unpaired cohort means. Original initialization scores remain visible separately.

Planned scientific game count: nine initialization cohorts + nine trained midpoint + nine trained endpoint + nine frozen midpoint + nine frozen endpoint, each 800 games = 36,000, plus bounded calibration/anchor diagnostics. No hidden reduction from 100 total games to 25 per matrix cell. If that floor does not fit, the scientific plan is unadmitted; do not launch a smaller cohort and call it accepted evidence.

Hardware and budget — proposed, not reserved

Choose a local CPU run after approved laptop cohorts release their resource, using configured hardware and host/evaluator leases. No Mini slot (ETU-125 owns its separate selection), no GPU rental and no displaced existing work. Proposed ceiling: 48 occupied wall hours and 64 additive process hours, at most one single-thread learner and one single-thread evaluator. No dollar allocation; report dollars unavailable unless an explicit host price is frozen. This is a budget proposal only, not permission to wait/queue/start work automatically.

Reserve process time before admission: calibration 1 h, scientific learning 18 h (2 h maximum per pool/seed), evaluation 36 h, setup/export/report/recovery 9 h. All attempts, failures, clean-play/replay and artifact transfer count. Wall and process ceilings both bind; overlap is not free. No carryover of another Task's budget. Future explicit authorization and resource availability are required.

Calibration would require separate authorization; its proposed bounded phase is: all three pools, a fourth seed 12250, same model/streams/rule, up to 100 updates per pool, reload/complete games in each matrix cell against both anchors, plus greedy vs random and same-anchor checks. Use at most one hour total; failures stay retained. No checkpoint from calibration enters scored science. Record setup/export/replay, per-update block timing and per-cell game time, not bare forward-pass rates.

Resolve N = largest multiple of 100 no greater than 10,000 for which a 1.5× margin on the slowest measured per-update block cost fits every 2 h learner cap. Also require the full 36,000-game projection (1.5× slowest per-cell measured mean, with measured setup/replay costs) to fit the 36 h evaluation reservation, and full projected critical path to fit 48 h. N < 1,000 or incomplete calibration means budget-infeasible, not a smaller silent launch. Timings extrapolate; they do not promise sustained throughput. Commit resolved counts, absolute milestones, cutoffs and receipts before any scientific score is revealed.

Game watchdog: 120 seconds/10,000 Commands, unchanged across pools. Timeouts, nonfinite losses, illegal Commands, hidden-truth dependence, identity drift or replay mismatch retain the attempt and mark the affected cohort incomplete. An incomplete cell gets no rate; missing cells/seeds suppress pooled method claims. No best-seed replacement, early strength stopping or automatic restart. Recover only an explicitly admitted exact state within the original budget; otherwise stop with missing/censored evidence. Report engineering failures apart from negative learning. Endpoint is last planned raw checkpoint, not best score.

Design detail · Alternatives, failure modes and implementation sequence

Local design draft · refreshed 13 October 2026

Rejected: legacy numeric-only simple models versus semantic full models (changes representation); stripping abilities from authored cards (new rules pretending to be old); independently shrunken policy catalogs (different parameterization); reusing default fingerprints/IDs; mixed mirror training (ETU-125's experiment); or a second private runner/store. The chosen fixed-catalog route costs explicit admission work but isolates the learner and preserves the real full world.

Wild success: a reproducible simple-game learning curve becomes an inexpensive positive learning diagnostic, and highlights where the full game warrants a focused next experiment. Wild failure: catalog admission silently changes rules, weak anchors reward artifacts, or evaluation consumes the entire budget. Exact bindings, per-cell controls and all-in calibration address those failures; three seeds and two simple anchors still cannot establish general playing strength.

  1. This slice — design/capture only. Resolve scientific intent, cards, identity gaps and proposed admission/budget. No implementation or calibration.
  2. Identity and matrix prerequisite. Reuse/adapt ETU-125's matrix if delivered; otherwise extend the existing arena/protocol. Bind finite setup rosters, native simple content and one common policy catalog through ordinary checkpoint admission. Add the four-stream cross-role assignment contract to ordinary collection/reset/recovery and verify per-cell transition quotas. Preserve authored full-world behavior and old readers.
  3. Reproducible declaration/report software. Add the typed complexity protocol, side-effect-free plan export, frozen-control evaluation, calibrated count resolver and evidence-only notebook/HTML. Reuse execution/publication.
  4. Only under a later bounded authorization: native/software admission, calibration, pre-registration commit and unique launch. Finish all declared cohorts or report incompleteness; publish originals and analysis. Scientific and chapter acceptance remain separate from software delivery.

Delete — do not maintain: no code deleted in this design slice. In the later cutover, replace the single-catalog construction assumption in SemanticCardEncoder and selected-match-only routing in the shared monitoring path with explicit validated bindings. Preserve the historical authored recipe and four-leg evidence reader as supported contracts, not competing execution implementations. Do not add a private subset encoder, ID alias table or a second matrix evaluator. No frozen plans, reports, catalog bytes or receipts are removed.
