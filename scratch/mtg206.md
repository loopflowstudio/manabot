## Accepted outcome and boundaries

and renders all page filter combinations from those rollups with Etude's batched
intervals. Per-card results must equal the previous query apart from interval
draws. Completion includes both the converted MTG-183 u005950-versus-heuristic
page rendering in seconds and one real L4 evaluation costing at most $1 whose
blocks support the page and replay audit (Jack Heart, 2026-10-27).

The SDK must be a released version containing EXP-30; no vendored bootstrap.
Conversion of retained bucket data and deletion of historical per-file objects
are separate work, outside this Task. Existing evidence and world admission
remain unchanged.

## Shape (implemented)

- `card_metrics.CARD_ROLLUPS`: `card_deals` (card_counts ⋈ card_plays, by card,
  deal, on_play, deck, opponent_deck, source_commit, player, opponents; where
  eligible; gp/oh/gd/gih/gns and `_won`) and `game_deals` (card_plays, same
  splits plus eligible, outcome; `wins`).
- `card_evaluation`: `play_card_match` and `trace_card_tables` return
  `etude.eval.match.Result`; the replay worker's evidence files are a scratch
  handoff (`_handed_over` reads, verifies, deletes). `evaluate_cards` holds with
  `rollups=CARD_ROLLUPS` and a temporary scratch folder; rows drop `evidence`.
- `card_query`: `card_db` = `statsdb(..., rollups=CARD_ROLLUPS)`;
  `card_variants(db, selection, dimensions)` computes every combination in one
  grouping-sets pass for card counts, with summaries from `game_deals`
  (single-valued dimensions are not split);
  `card_data` is its empty combination. GIH intervals: one
  `estimate(deals, wins, opportunities)` per combination (etude-sdk 0.2.10,
  EXP-30). Only GIH expands to deal-by-card matrices; other metrics sum
  directly by card. `_CardBlocks` names the aligned input arrays.
  `load_game`/`audit` use `read_blobs`.
- `card_page.page_data` = `card_variants` over all five page dimensions.
  Empty selections return no variants and render an empty page; run, commit,
  deck and opponent-deck filters are covered by regression tests.
  Exclusions-only variants retain runs and filter choices; zero eligible games
  show unavailable rates and their exclusion summary, separately from absent
  selections and card search/minimum filters hiding rows.
- `mtg186_cards` returns `Result`, declares rollups, hands evidence through
  scratch. `elemental_long_eval.done_keys` reads sealed blocks.
- `experiments/mtg206_cards.py`: the small L4 smoke (`evaluate_cards` on the
  `cards verify` schedule, 96 games, run `mtg206-cards-l4-verify`, experiment
  `mtg206-cards`, ≤ $1).

## Concept review — 2026-10-27

Review outcome after the correction: **nothing further to change and no open
product choice within MTG-206**. The Task description returned by
`lf task status MTG-206 --json` agrees with the accepted boundaries above.
`scratch/concept-review.html` now shows the current review, replacing its stale
“implementation pending” presentation. This is a review-record update, not a
new implementation direction or a waiver of acceptance.

The earlier review reproduced `page_data` discarding excluded selections.
Keeping those selections follows the documented exclusion-reporting contract;
it is not a new decision attributed to Jack Heart. The simpler experience is:
choose an experiment and cohort, read its eligible rates and exclusions, then
change filters or audit its evidence. The experiment pools runs; the selection
chooses player-games; each match owns its evidence. Rollups and blocks are
derived query data and delivery machinery, not additional user lifecycles.

The correction is implemented: all-excluded and mixed cohorts retain provenance,
selectable decks/positions, exclusions and null rates through `page_data` and
`write_page`. Absent combinations remain absent; grouped results equal standalone
queries. The page distinguishes no retained games, no eligible games, and card
filters hiding rows. `docs/card-evaluation.md` describes these states.

Across layers, `CardSelection` selects the rollups, `card_variants` derives the
results, and `page_data` embeds them for browser selection without recomputing
metrics. `Result(tables, blobs)` removes the separately managed evidence-file
lifecycle; temporary worker files remain an internal handoff. No further
record, API, storage migration or usage/skill instruction change is indicated.

Counterexamples remain binding: a nonexistent cohort must not acquire invented
games; an exclusions-only cohort must not disappear; eligible rates in a mixed
cohort must stay equal to standalone queries. The current query, page and
existing regression fixtures address all three. Resume retains completed
matches and retries failed ones under identical players and conditions.

Recovery still means changing filters or auditing retained evidence. Missing
blobs require restoring original delivered bytes and repeating audit; rollup
availability does not certify evidence availability. No visual proof is claimed.
The exact-pair timing remains required independently of this correction. The
next proof is still the final query's 363-combination page on the original
converted u005950-versus-heuristic cohort, followed by the remaining gate checks.
At this review, missing original data was an evidence dependency, not an unresolved
